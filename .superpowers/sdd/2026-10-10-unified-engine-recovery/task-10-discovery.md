### Combat catalogue safety is an early dependency

`RpcPublishedCombatContentSource.readCatalogueSnapshot()` explicitly rejects any kind outside the five stored kinds. `resolveCombatContentCatalogueSnapshot()` also throws on unknown kinds. `RpcCombatContentAuthoringStore.#validatePublication()` compiles the whole prospective catalogue through that resolver and timing compatibility checker. SQL `read_combat_content_catalogue_v1()` returns **all** current publication kinds. Simply extending SQL checks and inserting a Discipline/Tag/Item publication would therefore break authoring and battle admission.

Keep one content repository and one immutable record identity/history, but separate the **typed authoring snapshot** from its **compatible combat projection**. The full snapshot parser validates every recognized kind; a deliberate projection feeds only actual combat executable kinds into the existing combat compiler. Known noncombat containers/future records must never reach `validatePublishedSkill` or become castable. Truly unknown/malformed kinds still fail closed, rather than being silently ignored. Catalogue tokens must continue protecting the complete prospective change, including relationship choices and lifecycle states, not a weakened combat-only subset. Keep existing service-only current/exact reads and immutable captures working. A future draft alone has no current/runtime effect.
