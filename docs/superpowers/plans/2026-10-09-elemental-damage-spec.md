Current typed Storm Skills provide that debuff by default; explicit authored Conductive effects must not accidentally duplicate applications. Default bonus is the existing 20%. Expose Water's Drenched Storm bonus and Storm's Conductive bonus in Master, including validation, immutable publish/restore and copy propagation.

Unit effects must not occur on allied, missed, immune, invalid or dead targets. Repeated damage packets must not consume a newly applied charge again in the same command. Forecast and actual execution share the same result.

Respect explicit Master timing overrides and describe the captured lifetime accurately; expose authored duration through existing Skill authoring patterns when practical. Follow established status timing/copy/stacking contracts. Status removal/expiry restores Initiative and facing permission immediately.

Re-sort the unacted remainder of a round at a safe command/turn transition; never interrupt the active actor, grant an extra turn, skip an eligible living actor or revive a dead/summon-expired actor. At subsequent round boundaries sort all eligible actors by effective Initiative, restoring base order when modifiers expire. Keep persistent seeded ties, pending summons and existing tempo offsets.
