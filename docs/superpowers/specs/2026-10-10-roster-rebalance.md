## Mechanical budget and honesty

- Preserve100 AP, resource/attribute/Defense formulas, turn and round rules, source privacy and current elemental policy. Costs and individual roster definitions are the balance controls.
- Price ordinary attacks around40–60 AP, narrower utility around25–45 AP and signature Essences around55–75 AP. These are design bands, not an exemption from per-record simulation. An unusually cheap or expensive entry needs a concrete opportunity-cost rationale in its manifest row.
- Basic Move stays20 AP per terrain point; Basic Attack and Guard stay30 AP; both recovery choices stay50 AP/10% with the shared two-owner-turn recovery cooldown. These are reviewed universal baselines. Their registry records remain fully editable; no cosmetic new version is required solely to claim that they were considered.
- Attached Move is included in its Skill's overall price. Its path, entered-tile cap, remaining Movement, terrain, Rooted, Jump and reached-origin restrictions remain real. Never add a walking surcharge.
- Costs are explicit AP/MP/HP entries. HP payment leaves1 HP, is not damage and cannot trigger a damage reaction. Every ordinary Manual action pays AP. A free Controlled prime is once per character/ability/battle; it is neither a free release nor a repeatedly renewable resource.
- Budget complete maximum outcomes, not just each packet's printed Power. Splitting one attack into several packets must not silently multiply its offensive-stat scaling. Native current damage normally receives the standard2500-basis-point Power coefficient; canonical damage must specify its intended scaling explicitly. Secondary conditional packets may use an explicit zero coefficient when their purpose is a small fixed bonus. Canonical flat healing, Barrier and MP changes must be budgeted as their actual fixed values; they do not silently inherit a native envelope's recovery scaling.
- Ordinary Physical and Mystic offensive groups should respect the existing20/16 authored-Power expectations across their possible packets, unless a specifically costed signature exception is justified and tested. Evaluate real scaled damage, accuracy, defense, criticals, status resistance, facing and the140% hostile direct-damage policy in legal builds. A parser-valid magnitude alone does not establish balance.
- Percentage recovery uses actual resource caps, clock, application count and existing recovery modifiers. Up-front healing, scheduled healing and a shield are different choices. Do not give every class the same heal at a different percentage.
- Ongoing benefits require a meaningful maintained state, an equipped-slot cost and a clear counterplay window. Automatic damage/recovery requires finite usage/cooldown and resource budgets. Test no input, repeated receipts, terminal state and simultaneous applications; no endlessly self-funding reaction chain.
- Preserve exact Fire/Ice/Water/Storm companion rules and authored order. Chilled is the public name for `frozen`; Frozen Ground is separate. Do not claim that a copied status consumes its donor or that a generic status removal has a narrower capability than its actual typed payload.
- Copy Buffs copies eligible positive effects from the selected donor to the actor. Copy Debuffs copies the actor's eligible negative effects to the selected recipient. Donors remain unchanged. Copy is first, single-unit and Manual; any subsequent cleanse/dispel must be explicit and legal.
- Automatic event nodes observe battle events. Do not describe a trigger as owner-only unless causal binding and legal targeting actually enforce that. For an own-action reaction, a unit target restricted to Self with the triggering subject can enforce ownership; a `self` target alone ignores that binding. Resource-threshold crossings must use the owner's real immutable mutation frame.
- Names and descriptions explain typed mechanics; they never invent a handler, a narrower recipient, resource transfer, mark consumption, elemental conversion or positional return.

## Resonance design standard

All136 pairs receive an individual authored decision and interaction rationale. Shared builder code is acceptable; repeating a generic bonus-damage-plus-recovery recipe with a different name or percentage is not. Preserve pair IDs and pair ownership while replacing generic presentation names where a more precise name explains the actual mechanic.

Use a deliberate mixture of command modifiers, bounded automatic reactions, maintained conditional benefits and selected active hybrid actions. Each pair needs a setup/payoff/counterplay account, honest cost/clock, at least one failing-before/final-outcome acceptance case for any new extension it uses, and no-input/replay reasoning. Not every pair needs every activation mode.

An active or Controlled Resonance is not publishable merely because the kernel accepts its definition. The current mixed-build special card labels Resonance Passive and has no action artwork button. Any selected active design requires a complete server/controller/forecast/reader/keyboard/touch integration in that existing footprint, with the same ready-only overlay rule. Manual modifier definitions likewise require an actual explicit player selection flow, never implicit attachment.

## Existing mechanics that need a complete canonical path when selected

- Conditional and Automatic candidates require an honest usable Master preview. A fixed healthy target cannot demonstrate low-HP triage, and a direct Manual quote cannot simulate an Automatic damage-threshold reaction. Provide bounded typed scenario controls and actual engine-issued event simulation, with exact costs/outcomes and no fabricated legal receipt. Automatic-only equipped Skills also require a truthful non-cast cockpit state. Candidate rows flag these requirements until implemented.
- Canonical Summon is now supported by the saved shared-command implementation, with one final single-tile Manual spawn. It retains native profile, pending, visibility, deferred initiative, AI and lifetime behavior.
- Three current Skills carry persistent Ground definitions: Wildwarden Venom Shot, Cinderweaver Flame Burst and Frostweaver Chilling Mist. Their records cannot be converted by silently dropping that metadata. Retain/rework their actual role through an explicit validated captured group Ground contract and the existing Ground execution path, or make a deliberate documented design change. An instantaneous Ground-targeted attack is not evidence of a persistent area.
- An enemy positional-return loop is a candidate, not an implicit extension of self Rewind. If selected, first define recipient/anchor, clock, duration/refresh, blocked landing, visibility, cleanse/counterplay and no-input/replay behavior. Keep its content unpublished until every real handler/editor/capture/reader/AI path passes.
- Pierce's future equipment Armor boundary remains distinct from Physical/Mystic Defense. Its meaningful current test is damage plus zero extra bypass when explicit equipment Armor is absent; never claim that a current defensive stat was bypassed. Classify this future capability honestly in coverage.
