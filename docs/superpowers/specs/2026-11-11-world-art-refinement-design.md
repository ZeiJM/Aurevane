# World art refinement

Nick asked to implement all remaining art findings after the verified #1036 release. The visual target remains the supplied winter village and river/biome references: inhabited, painterly, natural, readable at ordinary zoom. This is a new source pass on main230b0870, not a replay of that release.

Use the existing Canvas renderer and original compressed art. Improve cached terrain and scenery composition rather than replace topology or add another world state. Keep full-sized towns, no route line, biome-specific wildlife outside towns, existing movement/water authority, coordinates, player state, historical captures, live settings and Owner drafts. Public/loaded/unknown and private/surveyed boundaries must remain distinct. No migrations, hosted fixtures or content republication.

The work covers natural-looking public and inland banks, worn irregular remote paths, wider coherent biome blending, district-specific settlement dressing, snowy hamlet building variants, varied private Void fringes and pool edges, and weather/motion/occlusion evidence. Major towns gain regional supporting compositions around their existing functional anchors. Hamlet identity combines existing four building families with bounded region/anchor-specific working yards; this is not a promise of 72 new painted town layouts. Leave all interaction anchors and crossing decks unchanged. Cosmetic sprites introduce no movement blocks; existing legal ground and blocked water/building footprints remain unchanged.

Implementation choice: retain server-issued cell geometry and use cosmetic sediment, worn edges, shoreline vegetation and fog contours inside existing known cells. Do not depict new walkable land or carve away legal ground. Cache static detail. Keep moving particles and scenery within existing quality/reduced-motion budgets. Snow building art uses the same 2x2 crops/anchors as the current four-family atlas, loaded only in Frostmere, with existing-art fallback.

Alternatives considered: changing world hydrology/layout would change gameplay and exceeds this art task; merely recolouring existing square fills would leave the demonstrated problem. This pass uses layered irregular material boundaries and authored regional compositions in the existing renderer.

Acceptance: focused geometry/render/authority-preservation guards; actual decoding and image readiness; configured exact-head checks; authenticated native captures of towns, all72hamlets, all13Void districts/rare views, representative bank/biome seams and controlled fixture weather plus movement/occlusion. Native fixture weather is visual evidence only, not production weather acceptance. Physical-handset and human acceptance must stay open until actually performed. No new Preview/Production is triggered by implementation. The prior one-release authorization was consumed by #1036; this request authorizes the new implementation/review branch.
