## Canonical model and shared interfaces

New `combat-definition.ts` owns `AbilityDefinition`, `AbilityBehavior`, `AbilityEffect`, `AbilityTargeting`, `AbilitySelection`, `RequirementNode`, `CombatAccuracyRule` and parser result/error types. Existing content envelopes keep identity, immutable content version, source, media and presentation. Their optional `ability` property is canonical when present; legacy execution fields are adapters, never a second independent source of mechanics. Basic definitions use the same model. Containers hold references or supported behaviors and do not become castable merely because they have a content category.

`AbilityDefinition` has schemaVersion 1 and one or more stable-ID behaviors. Each behavior declares Manual/Automatic/Ongoing activation, action/modifier mode, Attack/Recovery/Utility classification, explicit AP/MP/HP costs, cooldown, requirements, targeting where applicable, ordered effects and private accuracy where relevant. Ordinary Skills use one behavior. Multiple Manual groups require an explicit group selection; the client cannot silently choose a different group. Resource values are nonnegative safe integers, percentages are integer basis points, damage power is 1–20, tile/count/turn/round fields retain their own units. Require finite bounded arrays and reject unknown mechanical keys.

`RequirementNode` is a typed All/Any tree with depth 8 and total node budget 128, evaluated against owner/selected/affected/triggering subjects where supported. Predicates distinguish resource state from threshold crossing, event type/phase, action qualification, status presence and prime presence. Missing event subjects fail closed. Intrinsic Skill source qualifies mechanics; selecting another earned Discipline eligibility does not reparent the Skill.

`combat-tag-registry.ts` connects each supported stable tag ID to its payload fields, units, recipients, timing/stacking compatibility, handler, concise/full explanation and runtime capability. Registry-owned mechanics cannot be changed through dictionary text. Versioned Tag metadata may change presentation only. Existing supported handlers are reused. Unsupported new tag behavior, Automatic/Ongoing combinations and future activation fail with a named error before publication.

Accuracy uses `{kind:'standard'}` or `{kind:'fixed',chanceBasisPoints:number}` with integer 0–10000. Fixed chance ignores ordinary Accuracy/Evasion adjustments without bypassing legality, immunity or mitigation. One applicable recipient hit result is shared by hit-dependent packets; independent proc chances remain separate. Ordinary combat Accuracy/Evasion statistics and authorized forecasts/results remain legitimate public data. Canonical/legacy hit-authoring fields and materialized private actions are recursively omitted from every public transport.

## Move, Basics and Controlled

Move is a registered executable effect, not a prose tag. It is permitted once, first, in a Manual walking group. Choose a Ground destination/path, traverse legal orthogonal tiles, then perform any compatible follow-up using the actual reached position; an interruption cannot use the planned endpoint as the attack origin. Preserve terrain, occupancy, Rooted, Jump, Airborne and Poison step behavior. A defeated actor cannot perform later effects. Authored ability costs pay once, walking AP pays per entered tile. Authored traversal AP is 10–100 per traversal point (default 20), actual entered tiles remain at least 10 AP, and an authored 1–20 tile cap also obeys the actor's remaining Movement. Move is not enabled for Automatic/Ongoing or human summon commands without a later real extension.

Basic Move is editable through this same definition. Its zero-authored-AP offensive follow-up can commit once per acting turn, including a miss; invalid intent and free priming do not consume that bound. Positive-authored-AP use follows ordinary repetition. Pure walking remains repeatable within AP/Movement. Basic Attack retains the existing higher committed Physical/Mystic Attack family and stat-derived damage (Physical ties); publication requires positive AP until a separately implemented bounded extension is reviewed. Stable content identity `unarmed.basic` is distinct from its command identity. Both Basics are captured at encounter creation and their actual safe captures reach availability, forecasts and public readers.

Controlled primes the selected whole Manual group, captures exact source/group/version and Rewind's position anchor, and reconciles prime-dependent Ongoing state. Ready/blocked is recalculated from legal current intent. Legal release commits once; rejected release retains the prime. Cancel, defeat, source removal and battle end clean up the relevant source-owned state. Temporary inability does not discard a prime. Rewind restores position only, through passability/occupancy/Rooted checks, with no resource/history rollback. Multiple Manual choices, native keyboard, stale requests, reconnect and PvE/PvP must share the real controller/service path.
