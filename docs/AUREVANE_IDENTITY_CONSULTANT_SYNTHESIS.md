# AUREVANE — Identity Consultant Synthesis

**Status:** Owner-approved authoritative creative-direction refinement.  
**Direction approved:** 2026-09-04.  
**Authority:** Subordinate to `docs/GAME_MASTER_PLAN.md`, `docs/LORE_BIBLE.md`, and specific mechanical/lore authorities. Newer than and complementary to `docs/AUREVANE_IDENTITY_STANDARD.md` and `docs/LORE_CONTENT_IDENTITY_FRAMEWORK.md`.

This document records the final synthesis between AUREVANE's internal identity pass and an adversarial external originality review.

It does **not** copy the external review into canon wholesale. It preserves ideas that strengthen AUREVANE, modifies ideas that would overreach current canon, and rejects proposals that would prematurely solve protected mysteries, invent unsupported metaphysics, or replace one fantasy cliché with another.

The governing creative goal remains:

> **Becoming has meaning because continuity and consequence remain.**

And the permanent originality test remains:

> **Could this appear essentially unchanged in another fantasy RPG?**
>
> If yes: **What makes this version unmistakably AUREVANE without merely giving it a stranger proper noun?**

---

# 1. Final strategic judgment

AUREVANE does **not** need a stranger setting.

It needs a more consistently specific one.

The project may use familiar fantasy scaffolding — forests, mountains, dragons, swords, merchants, ruins, monsters, guilds, roads, towns — when that scaffolding helps players understand the world quickly.

Originality must come primarily from:

- what societies believe is worth preserving or changing;
- how those beliefs affect work, law, repair, mourning, trade and family life;
- how history leaves material layers rather than exposition-only lore;
- how meaningful player choices remain acknowledged later;
- how buildcraft expresses Becoming mechanically;
- how contradiction is evidenced rather than random;
- how the world responds differently to Soul States without reducing them to factions;
- how Aurevane can correctly identify specific injustice while reaching a catastrophic universal conclusion;
- how visual and audio motifs recur with history rather than functioning as palette labels.

The target is **recognizable relationships**, not maximum novelty density.

---

# 2. Ten anti-generic pressure points

Future content review should actively check these risks.

## 2.1 Biome-as-culture

A volcanic region is not automatically a fire culture.

A frozen region is not automatically a Viking culture.

A forest region is not automatically a druid culture.

A desert region is not automatically a mystical merchant culture.

Biome creates pressures. Culture is an accumulated response to history, institutions, resources, migration, belief and conflict.

## 2.2 Great Vanes becoming collectible MacGuffins

The Eight Great Vanes must never collapse into an eight-dungeon crystal hunt.

People may depend on:

- old roads;
- civic institutions;
- observatories;
- waterworks;
- archives;
- shrines;
- inherited customs;
- structures unusually resistant to Drift;

that are later discovered to have some relationship to Vane influence.

But ordinary communities must **not** generally know that they are operating "Vane infrastructure."

The Vane relationship remains hidden inside believable civilization until earned by the story.

## 2.3 Veyr becoming one mysterious outsider culture

The Veyr must contain:

- regional and lineage differences;
- competing political interests;
- incompatible religious interpretations;
- people who value stable-world civilization;
- people who distrust it;
- people who disagree about Aurevane;
- people who are correct about some continuity phenomena and wrong about others.

No single Veyr elder, faction, or settlement explains "the Veyr view."

## 2.4 Recurring NPCs becoming quest furniture

Important NPCs should continue living outside the player's current objective.

Their lives may involve:

- changing professions;
- marriages and friendships;
- rivalries;
- family obligations;
- political opinions;
- failures;
- changed beliefs;
- movement between settlements;
- altered responses to the player after world events or Rekindling.

At least some recurring NPCs should remain deliberately ordinary and low-stakes.

A recurring character does not require a cosmic secret.

## 2.5 Ruins becoming magic-themed loot containers

Most ruins should be historical places first.

They need:

- an ordinary original function;
- later reuse;
- material alterations;
- incomplete interpretation;
- a relationship to present people.

Continuity contradiction is powerful specifically because it is uncommon.

## 2.6 Soul States becoming faction A versus faction B

**Soul State** is a persistent personal condition, not a political team identifier.

Soulmarked and Soul-Severed people may organize socially, religiously or professionally in some contexts, but there is no default requirement for:

- two global factions;
- Soul State armies;
- Soul State PvP teams;
- one universal Soulmarked culture;
- one universal Severed culture.

Regional institutions and individuals may react differently.

## 2.7 Aurevane becoming obviously evil or secretly correct

Both flatten the story.

Aurevane should repeatedly be capable of:

1. identifying a real and specific injustice;
2. understanding why the victim is hurt;
3. offering genuine compassion;
4. correctly exposing hypocrisy;
5. then extending that insight into an illegitimate universal conclusion about limitation, exclusion or finality.

The seam between correct diagnosis and catastrophic universalization is where much of her danger lives.

## 2.8 Disciplines becoming renamed classes

Each mature Discipline must possess a clear **combat proposition** — a distinctive way of solving tactical problems — rather than merely a stat profile and themed ability icons.

When appropriate, that proposition may also echo a philosophy of action: patience, commitment, adaptation, protection, risk, tempo, control, sacrifice, etc.

Do **not** force every Discipline into a philosophical monologue.

The standard is mechanical identity first.

Primary/Secondary mixing should create a legible new tactical relationship, not only numerical optimization.

## 2.9 Continuity phenomena becoming generic glitch horror

Do not default to:

- datamosh aesthetics;
- purple corruption;
- static;
- flickering polygons;
- "anything can happen" monsters.

A continuity phenomenon must have a stable rule players can observe, learn and sometimes exploit.

## 2.10 Fantasy naming becoming the identity substitute

Naming systems should be culturally coherent, pronounceable and historically layered.

Do not make every culture follow one cute naming gimmick.

Examples such as inherited witness-titles, grafted names, funerary renaming or provisional names remain **possible local traditions**, not universal regional laws unless later canon explicitly approves them.

# 3. Content portfolio balance

The existing `60/30/10` instinct remains useful as a **creative balance heuristic, never a production quota**:

- most local content should concern ordinary people and local life;
- a meaningful minority should express regional history, institutions and material culture;
- a smaller memorable layer should touch central continuity/cosmic material when the story tier supports it.

Do not impose a rigid rule such as "50% of all quests must be non-Aurevane."

Instead, every major content batch should be reviewed for **mystery concentration**.

If a starter settlement's content makes Aurevane, the Vanes, Soul States or continuity failures feel like the only things anyone cares about, the batch is over-centralized.

If a late Unmoored Zone contains almost no continuity material, it is under-expressing its purpose.

Context controls the balance.

---

# 4. Ordinary-content requirement

Each substantial settlement or regional content package should include some content whose appeal is simply that it is good world content.

Useful forms include:

- friendship;
- local competition;
- apprenticeship;
- animal care;
- repair work;
- a festival;
- family disagreement;
- comedy;
- investigation with a factual answer;
- trade disputes;
- rescue;
- exploration;
- harmless rivalry;
- local sport or game;
- food or craft tradition.

This is not filler.

Ordinary content establishes the baseline the cosmic story can later threaten or recontextualize.

### Ambient dialogue rule

Routine ambient NPC barks should usually be concrete and human.

Avoid making ordinary market vendors, guards and travelers speak like philosophers merely because the setting has philosophical themes.

Abstract thematic dialogue belongs where a character, institution or story has earned it.

---

# 5. Regional production rule

Do not deep-build all eight regions simultaneously.

Before deep production, all eight should have enough scaffolding to prove:

- they are not monocultures;
- their internal disagreement exists;
- their visual/audio identity is not palette-only;
- they contain ordinary livelihoods;
- they have at least one conflict unrelated to the central mystery.

Then use **vertical slices** to prove the authoring standard before multiplying content.

A strong first regional vertical slice should contain:

- one major settlement;
- one smaller settlement or route community;
- a recurring NPC ensemble;
- an ordinary quest;
- a regional-values quest;
- one creature from ordinary ecology;
- one culturally entangled creature;
- one layered ruin with no cosmic explanation;
- one disputed historical object/site;
- at most one continuity-facing mystery beat where appropriate;
- representative art/audio identity.

The purpose is to prove the content grammar, then scale it.

---

# 6. Bestiary production contract

AUREVANE's bestiary should maintain four broad content sources:

1. **ordinary ecology**;
2. **culturally entangled creatures**;
3. **historically shaped creatures**;
4. **rare continuity-affected creatures**.

The first production proof should include at least one creature from each category before large-volume bestiary expansion.

## 6.1 Continuity-affected creature rule

Every continuity-affected creature needs:

- a stable core creature identity;
- one bounded continuity rule;
- material or behavioral evidence of the rule;
- gameplay consequence;
- an observable limit;
- a reason the phenomenon occurs in that location or story tier.

"Reality is weird here" is not sufficient design.

### Over-consistency is an approved subtype, not the universal rule

The external review proposed creatures that feel uncanny because they are **too consistent** rather than visibly glitching.

This is a strong AUREVANE-specific direction and should be explored.

Examples may include:

- anatomy preserving evidence of two seasonal histories;
- behavior fitting two formerly incompatible habitats unusually well;
- repeated route-memory that matches an old map no longer physically correct.

But do **not** require all continuity creatures to follow over-consistency.

Other bounded contradiction types may exist if they remain legible, evidenced and setting-coherent.

---

# 7. Archaeology confidence model

Important historical discoveries should distinguish between what people know and how strongly they know it.

Useful authoring labels are:

```text
CERTAIN
Material/documentary evidence is strong and mutually consistent.

PROBABLE
The best explanation is strongly supported but incomplete.

DISPUTED
Multiple plausible interpretations compete; ordinary historical uncertainty is enough to explain this.

CONTRADICTORY
Authentic evidence supports mutually incompatible histories beyond ordinary explanation.
```

These labels are primarily an **authoring and Archive design tool**. They do not need to appear literally on every player-facing tooltip.

Important rule:

> **DISPUTED is not the same thing as CONTRADICTORY.**

A disputed ruin can remain completely non-supernatural.

That distinction protects the impact of genuine continuity evidence.

---

# 8. Ruin portfolio proof

Before producing a large ruin catalog, prove at least these categories:

- an ordinary site with clear history and later reuse;
- an ordinary site whose purpose is genuinely disputed;
- a site with misleading propaganda but normal archaeology;
- a site with authentic contradictory evidence;
- a late-tier continuity site with rule-bound spatial/historical instability.

The player should learn not to assume "mystery = Aurevane."

---

# 9. Recurring NPC ensemble rule

Do not require two or three fully developed recurring NPCs in all eight regions before any other content can ship. That creates unnecessary front-loaded scope.

Instead, each major regional vertical slice should develop a **small ensemble** containing different narrative weights.

A strong ensemble might include:

- one character tied to a major local institution;
- one working professional with mundane concerns;
- one character who strongly disagrees with a regional norm;
- one traveler/migrant/outsider connecting regions;
- one deliberately low-stakes recurring character whose life is not secretly plot-important.

Across the broader game, recurring NPCs should include people who are:

- correct about something important;
- wrong about something important;
- capable of changing;
- incapable of changing in at least one meaningful respect;
- affected by events beyond the player's control.

The player is important, but the world must not wait motionless for them.

---

# 10. Soul State social design

Soul State is culturally significant without becoming a world-faction axis.

When later content legitimately activates the system, authors may explore:

- privacy around Soulmark manifestation;
- family pressure;
- religious interpretation;
- employment customs;
- military attitudes;
- marriage/family expectations;
- discrimination;
- prestige;
- legal protections;
- medical or scholarly study;
- Mantle traditions;
- misinformation and stereotypes.

However, the following are **not canon merely because they were suggested in external review**:

- Soul-Severed people cannot testify reliably;
- Soul-Severed people cannot swear ordinary oaths;
- Soul-Severed people are metaphysically incapable of normal continuity;
- Soulmarked people are inherently more trustworthy or less trustworthy;
- Soul State automatically determines civic eligibility.

Those would invent mechanics/metaphysics not established by current authority.

Any institutional restriction must have a specific cultural, historical, political or later-canon reason.

It may be unjust, pragmatic, superstitious, evidence-based, or mixed — but it cannot be asserted as a universal metaphysical fact without Owner approval.

# 11. Aurevane scene construction

For major Aurevane-adjacent arguments, use this pattern when appropriate:

```text
SPECIFIC HARM
What is actually hurting someone?

CORRECT DIAGNOSIS
What has Aurevane understood that defenders of the status quo refuse to admit?

HUMAN APPEAL
Why would a decent person find her answer emotionally compelling?

UNIVERSAL LEAP
Where does she turn one valid insight into a theory about all limitation, all exclusion, all finality or all chosen reality?

CONSEQUENCE
What identity, responsibility, history or personhood becomes impossible if her universal solution is applied consistently?
```

Examples of the pattern:

- unequal access to trustworthy witnesses does not prove that witnessed continuity itself is oppression;
- cruelly enforced mourning customs do not prove that no ending should ever be final;
- inherited obligations can be unjust without proving that no commitment should bind anyone;
- societies can mistreat Soul-Severed people without proving that irreversibility itself is the enemy.

Do not make every Aurevane scene structurally identical. This is a diagnostic tool, not a dialogue template.

---

# 12. Discipline identity refinement

AUREVANE's buildcraft is one of its strongest defenses against generic fantasy if content follows these rules.

Every mature Discipline should answer:

1. What tactical problem does this Discipline solve unusually well?
2. What decision does it repeatedly ask the player to make?
3. What does mastery look like in play rather than only in numbers?
4. What does an opponent learn to fear or respect about it?
5. What does its Essence express that mixed play gives up?
6. What kinds of Secondary pairings expose genuinely different uses of its tools?
7. What visual/audio behavior communicates the playstyle before the player reads the tooltip?

A Discipline that can be replaced by "warrior," "mage," or "rogue" without losing its decision grammar needs another design pass.

Do not create irreversible Discipline commitments merely for thematic symbolism. Approved Primary/Secondary and attunement rules remain the mechanical authority.

---

# 13. Hall of Selves guardrail

The Hall of Selves represents **lived, chosen character history**.

It must not populate from:

- hypothetical build previews;
- sandbox loadouts;
- temporary respec states;
- possibilities the character never actually committed to or lived through.

The Hall should feel meaningful because its entries represent completed or materially lived identities.

This principle does **not** move Hall of Selves implementation earlier in the roadmap.

Long-lead data needs should be considered when its proper phase approaches, but current Phase-3 buildcraft must not be expanded into premature Rekindling implementation.

---

# 14. City That Was Twice experiential goal

The City That Was Twice should eventually be experienced, not merely explained.

When its proper story/content phase arrives, the player should be able to encounter authentic contradictory evidence through:

- architecture;
- wear patterns;
- household objects;
- routes;
- civic records;
- burial evidence;
- NPC memory;
- tactical or exploration consequences.

The goal is for the player to personally conclude:

> **Both histories have evidence that should be impossible to fake.**

Do not reduce the City to a lore cutscene.

Do not implement the City early simply because it is identity-defining. It remains story-tier content governed by spoiler architecture.

---

# 15. Naming-system guardrail

Before mass-producing final proper nouns for a culture, establish a lightweight naming sheet covering:

- common phonetic tendencies;
- personal-name rhythm;
- family/community naming patterns;
- common titles;
- settlement-name construction;
- migration and loanword layers;
- names retained from older conquered/absorbed populations;
- pronunciation readability.

A cultural naming system should create **family resemblance**, not one mandatory gimmick.

For example:

- Aureth may have some witness-linked titles without every citizen carrying one;
- Verdant may include grafted-name traditions in some communities without making every person rename themselves;
- Hollow Coast may contain funerary renaming customs without making posthumous renaming universal.

---

# 16. Visual/audio refinement

Cross-world motifs must be assigned by **context**, not sprayed across all assets.

Useful registers:

- **roads/horizons** — travel, agency, choice, maps, distant possibility;
- **thresholds** — commitments, progression transitions, borders and entry;
- **repair/layering** — material history and continuity;
- **absence/negative space** — exclusion, missing evidence, unresolved history;
- **near-repetition with meaningful difference** — rare contradiction or memory motifs;
- **completed/incomplete forms** — used carefully around closure and becoming.

Do not reserve roads/horizons exclusively for or against Aurevane. Their meaning is older and broader than one simple villain symbol.

### Audio

Prefer functional regional sound identity over climate-only ambience.

Examples:

- work rhythms that become musical texture;
- practical chimes/signals;
- tide rhythms used socially;
- recurring instruments tied to local crafts;
- motifs returning in changed arrangement after world events;
- remembered melodies altered across generations;
- silence around irreversible commitments.

Sound should tell the player how people live there, not merely what the weather is.

---

# 17. Mechanical clarity without lore literacy

This is a permanent product rule:

> **A player must be able to understand what a mechanic does without understanding the metaphysics behind it.**

Examples:

- a Soul State tooltip must explain mechanical permanence/eligibility plainly before lore depth;
- Driftspace navigation must communicate reliable gameplay rules even if the player does not yet understand the Unchosen;
- a Discipline must teach its tactical role through gameplay/UI without requiring the player to read regional philosophy;
- a quest objective must remain understandable even if its historical interpretation is disputed.

Lore adds meaning.

Lore must not become a tax on basic usability.

---

# 18. Complexity ceiling

Do not stack every signature AUREVANE concept into one piece of content.

A normal quest should not require the player to simultaneously understand:

- regional politics;
- Soul State law;
- Veyr history;
- Vane architecture;
- Rekindling;
- Drift mechanics;
- Aurevane theology.

Complexity should be layered across the game.

A useful default is:

- one primary human problem;
- one secondary regional/system layer;
- optional deeper lore for players who seek it.

Late-story content may deliberately exceed this when the player's knowledge has earned it.

---

# 19. Approved production proof sets

Before large-scale Phase-5 world content, prove the identity standard with small representative sets.

## 19.1 Settlement proof

- one settlement with ordinary economy and material culture;
- one internal conflict unrelated to cosmic lore;
- one visible regional-value conflict;
- one recurring NPC ensemble;
- one change the player can cause that remains acknowledged.

## 19.2 Bestiary proof

- ordinary ecology;
- culturally entangled creature;
- historically shaped creature;
- rare continuity-affected creature.

## 19.3 Archaeology proof

- ordinary layered ruin;
- disputed non-supernatural site;
- authentic contradictory site.

## 19.4 Quest-tone proof

- straightforward enjoyable quest;
- relationship quest;
- regional-values conflict;
- investigation with factual resolution;
- one consequential morally difficult quest;
- one mystery/continuity quest at the proper tier.

## 19.5 Presentation proof

- one environment where culture is recognizable without palette labels;
- one regional ambience where livelihood is audible;
- one recurring motif that changes after an event rather than merely looping forever.

# 20. Explicitly rejected or deferred external proposals

The following ideas are **not** adopted as current canon or implementation direction:

1. **Known communities consciously depending on a Great Vane as utility infrastructure.** Reworked: they may depend on ordinary sites/institutions later revealed to have Vane relationships.
2. **A hard numerical quota such as at least 50% non-Aurevane content.** Replaced with contextual portfolio balance and mystery-concentration review.
3. **All continuity creatures must be over-consistent.** Over-consistency is one strong subtype; the universal rule is bounded, evidenced contradiction.
4. **Soul-Severed people are metaphysically unable to witness, swear ordinary oaths, or participate in normal continuity.** Unsupported by canon and rejected unless a later approved system establishes a specific limitation.
5. **Soul State civic exclusions as universal law.** May exist locally for social/political reasons; not globally presumed.
6. **One naming gimmick per region.** Naming systems need internal variety and historical layers.
7. **Full 2–3 recurring-NPC casts for all eight regions before other content.** Too much upfront scope; use regional vertical slices and ensembles.
8. **Build Hall of Selves early because of long lead time.** Preserve the design requirement, not the premature implementation.
9. **Prototype the City That Was Twice immediately.** Protect it for its proper spoiler/story tier while recording the experiential goal.
10. **Make game-balance limits literal public expressions of Closed Horizon doctrine by default.** Thematic resonance is welcome, but balance constraints must not be over-explained through cosmology unless fiction explicitly supports it.

# 22. Final creative standard

The project should aim for three simultaneous reactions:

> **“I understand what this is.”**

because familiar fantasy scaffolding and clear mechanics remain usable;

> **“I have not seen this exact relationship before.”**

because culture, consequence, history and mechanics are specific;

and eventually:

> **“Of course this could only happen in AUREVANE.”**

That third reaction is the target.
