# AUREVANE — Long-Horizon Pacing Validation Policy

**Status:** Authoritative Owner-approved refinement of the numeric pacing policy in `docs/PROGRESSION_RETENTION.md` and `docs/ROADMAP.md`, subordinate only to `docs/GAME_MASTER_PLAN.md`.

**Direction approved:** 2026-08-16.

This document is intentionally narrow. It does **not** redesign Rekindling, Horizon progression, Wayfarer’s Practice, Veteran Edge, or the long-form character journey.

It clarifies one question:

> Is the current approximately 180-day / six-month production minimum an immutable design law, or a production pacing target that must be validated with real evidence?

The approved answer is:

> **180 days remains AUREVANE’s current production planning default, but the numeric value is an evidence-tested pacing hypothesis rather than an untouchable sacred constant.**

Where older wording in `docs/PROGRESSION_RETENTION.md`, `docs/ROADMAP.md`, or related planning can reasonably be read as saying that the exact 180-day number may never change even after representative long-term evidence, this later Owner-approved refinement controls **only that numeric-immutability question**.

All other long-horizon progression rules remain in force.

---

## 1. Invariant — AUREVANE Remains a Long-Form Character Game

The player’s first complete character era should still take **months**, not days or a couple of weekends.

Do not use this policy to turn AUREVANE into:

- a short XP sprint;
- a rapid prestige treadmill;
- a weekend-to-endgame game;
- a cash-accelerated progression race;
- an idle timer where the player simply waits for Rekindling eligibility.

The enduring principle is:

> **First-cycle completion is a long-form achievement made of meaningful character growth, mastery, world participation, build discovery and difficult accomplishments.**

---

## 2. Current Default

Until deliberately changed through an evidence-backed design decision, production planning continues to use:

- approximately **180 days / six calendar months** as the First Horizon / first Rekindling age target;
- gameplay milestone requirements in addition to character age;
- a long-form Rekindled-cycle target of similar magnitude unless later evidence supports another structure.

A dormant 180-day-old character is not automatically complete.

A highly active player cannot bypass the complete first-cycle journey merely by grinding XP faster.

---

## 3. What May Change

The exact numeric age/default may later move if real evidence supports it.

Illustratively, production might eventually conclude that:

- 150 days produces a healthier complete cycle;
- 180 days remains correct;
- 210 days is healthier because meaningful progression/content comfortably supports it;
- different Rekindling cycles warrant slightly different pacing envelopes.

Those examples are not recommendations.

The point is that the **number serves the game**. The game does not damage itself to preserve a number chosen before long-term cohorts existed.

---

## 4. Evidence Required for Material Change

Do not change the production minimum casually.

Before materially shortening or lengthening it, review evidence such as:

- progression simulations;
- accelerated QA/test-character progression;
- real cohort progression velocity;
- D1/D7/D30 and later retention where sample sizes make those metrics useful;
- return behavior by Horizon/progression band;
- time spent pursuing meaningful goals versus repetitive grind/waiting;
- Discipline Mastery and build-experimentation pacing;
- Soulmark/Confluence/equipment progression pacing;
- availability of serious PvE/PvP/story/world content well before final eligibility;
- Expedition and social participation;
- event/season cadence;
- player understanding of next goals;
- returning-player/catch-up experience;
- economy implications;
- PvP veteran-distribution implications;
- qualitative feedback from representative players who have actually lived through a meaningful portion of the journey.

Short-term tester impatience alone is not sufficient evidence.

Neither is internal attachment to the original number.

---

## 5. Serious Content Must Arrive Before Final Eligibility

The long-horizon gate must never be interpreted as:

- “the fun starts at day 180”;
- “hard Expeditions are forbidden until day 180”;
- “strong builds are forbidden until day 180”;
- “major story is forbidden until day 180”;
- “meaningful PvP is forbidden until day 180.”

Players should encounter increasingly serious content throughout the journey.

First Horizon/Rekindling marks completion of a full character era, not permission to finally access the game’s interesting systems.

---

## 6. Change-Control Rule

A material production pacing change should require:

1. evidence review;
2. explicit Owner approval;
3. simulation of progression/economy/PvP consequences;
4. versioned configuration change;
5. News/Manual communication if it affects live players materially;
6. migration/grandfathering policy where existing characters would otherwise be treated unfairly;
7. telemetry after the change;
8. rollback capability where practical.

Do not silently change a six-month journey into a four-month journey through an unnoticed balance constant.

---

## 7. Relationship to Wayfarer’s Practice

Wayfarer’s Practice remains absence protection.

Do not manipulate offline XP/Rested Momentum upward merely to force the cohort toward a new calendar target.

Pacing should emerge from the whole progression ecosystem, not from one catch-up mechanic.

---

## 8. Relationship to Monetization

No real-money product may accelerate the First Horizon/Rekindling calendar requirement, bypass required progression milestones, or sell a shorter cycle.

Evidence-adjustable pacing is a game-design operation, not a monetization opportunity.

---

## 9. Consolidation Requirement

This file exists because the Owner approved a targeted refinement after the earlier progression specification had already become large and authoritative.

At the next substantial progression-document consolidation, fold this policy into `docs/PROGRESSION_RETENTION.md` and `docs/ROADMAP.md` so the project returns to fewer canonical sources. Once that consolidation is complete, this file may be marked superseded/archived rather than remaining a permanent extra authority.

Until then, this document controls the numeric-immutability question described above.

---

## 10. Success Condition

The policy succeeds if AUREVANE preserves the feeling:

> **“My character has lived a real era in this world.”**

while remaining willing to adjust the exact calendar envelope when long-term evidence shows that doing so makes the game healthier, more rewarding, and more sustainable.
