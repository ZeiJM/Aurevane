# AUREVANE — Long-Horizon Progression, Rekindling & Retention

**Status:** Authoritative feature specification subordinate only to `docs/GAME_MASTER_PLAN.md`.

**Direction approved:** 2026-08-15.

This document defines AUREVANE's minimum six-month first-character journey, endgame pacing, healthy urgency/FOMO systems, repeatable prestige progression, PvP-safe veteran advantages, lore-discovery loop, and the Master Panel controls required to operate and rebalance those systems without routine code changes.

## 1. Product Goal

AUREVANE should feel like a world a player inhabits for months, not a game that can be solved in a weekend.

The first serious character journey from creation to first full endgame readiness should take **at least approximately six calendar months** for a highly engaged player. The game should remain rewarding throughout that period rather than hiding progress behind empty timers.

The target is not six months of repetitive grinding. It is six months of:

- character levels;
- Discipline mastery;
- Legacy and Confluence experimentation;
- Soulmark growth;
- equipment refinement;
- world/story progression;
- lore discovery;
- Expeditions;
- PvP seasons;
- guild and event participation;
- preparation for the first Rekindling.

A player should regularly feel that the character has meaningfully changed while still having important horizons ahead.

## 2. What "Six Months Minimum" Means

The first full journey is measured from character creation to **First Horizon / Rekindling eligibility**, not merely from level 1 to an arbitrary XP number.

A character cannot become fully first-cycle endgame-ready before the configured minimum character age, whose production default is **180 days**.

Endgame readiness requires all of the following categories, with exact thresholds controlled from the Master Panel:

- minimum character age;
- required character level;
- required story/world milestone;
- required number of mastered Disciplines;
- at least one completed advanced build milestone such as a meaningful Confluence path;
- Soulmark progression threshold;
- endgame PvE qualification such as Standard/Deep Expedition progression;
- an endgame rite/challenge that proves the player can use the build rather than merely wait out time.

Time alone is never sufficient. Power grinding alone is never sufficient.

## 3. Level 1–100 Pacing

The existing Level 1–100 model remains.

Production pacing should broadly target:

```text
Weeks 1–2       Levels 1–20
Weeks 3–6       Levels 21–40
Months 2–3      Levels 41–60
Months 3–4      Levels 61–75
Months 4–5      Levels 76–90
Months 5–6+     Levels 91–100 / first-cycle endgame
```

This is a target pacing envelope, not a promise that every player advances identically.

Fast and skilled players should progress more efficiently, discover more builds, complete harder content sooner, and earn more optional rewards. They should not be able to bypass the long-form character journey and reach full first-cycle completion in a few days.

## 4. Horizon Gates — Long-Term Pacing Without Daily Energy

AUREVANE should not use mandatory daily energy, arbitrary "come back in 8 hours" training buttons, or a dead-stop stamina system.

Instead, use **Horizon Gates**: broad character-age/progression bands that determine when the next major progression tier can open.

Example production defaults:

```text
HORIZON I      Day 0+       early world / Foundation growth
HORIZON II     Day 21+      intermediate progression
HORIZON III    Day 45+      advanced systems deepen
HORIZON IV     Day 75+      high-level regions/content
HORIZON V      Day 110+     veteran content
HORIZON VI     Day 150+     pre-endgame
FIRST HORIZON  Day 180+     level-100/endgame rite + Rekindling eligibility
```

Each Horizon also requires gameplay milestones, so a dormant six-month-old character does not automatically become endgame-ready.

The exact number, names, dates, level ceilings, milestone requirements, and enabled/disabled state are data-driven and Master Panel configurable.

## 5. Catch-Up and Absence Protection

The six-month structure should retain players without making life interruptions feel catastrophic.

Use:

- rested XP/Mastery bonuses;
- banked contracts that survive several missed days;
- accelerated progression below the player's currently unlocked Horizon;
- returning-player objectives;
- catch-up reward bundles that help a returning player resume, not leapfrog the entire journey;
- recurring access to important gameplay systems and build components.

A player who misses two weeks should feel behind on current happenings, not permanently ruined.

## 6. The Urgency / FOMO Engine

AUREVANE should deliberately create the emotional pull of:

> "Something is happening in the world and I would like to be there."

That is a valid retention tool when it is driven by world life, social history, discovery, prestige, and cosmetics rather than coercive punishment.

The game should use **experiential FOMO** aggressively enough that active players care about being present while avoiding permanent competitive damage for people who miss a window.

Core urgency systems include:

### Live world events

Events have real start/end windows, changing regions, encounters, NPCs, story states, bosses, music, and community objectives.

### First-witness recognition

Players present for the original occurrence of a major event may receive:

- Chronicle witness marks;
- profile badges;
- titles;
- commemorative cosmetics;
- event-specific portrait frames;
- guild/server history recognition.

These are prestige/social rewards, not permanent meta-defining combat power.

### Rotating rare encounters

Certain elites, merchants, anomalies, Expedition modifiers, lore fragments, or event variations may appear temporarily.

Important build-enabling rewards must eventually recur or have alternate acquisition paths.

### Seasons

PvP, world, guild, Expedition, and cosmetic rotations make each season feel distinct.

Season-limited prestige should focus on:

- ranked titles;
- banners;
- cosmetics;
- Chronicle entries;
- profile presentation;
- trophies;
- historical placement.

### Community races

Servers/guilds may race to:

- solve a lore puzzle;
- defeat a newly revealed boss;
- stabilize an event;
- discover a new Expedition branch;
- complete a world objective;
- reach a new Chronicle milestone.

### Event aftermath

Missing an event should still leave evidence in the world:

- changed NPC dialogue;
- Chronicle history;
- ruins or repaired locations;
- aftermath quests;
- archived documents;
- stories told by other players/NPCs.

This makes attendance feel meaningful without making absence equivalent to permanent account damage.

## 7. Explicit FOMO Guardrails

The owner may tune urgency strongly, but default production design must avoid cheap retention traps.

Do not make the primary form of FOMO:

- permanent combat power that can never return;
- a daily streak whose loss destroys weeks of value;
- mandatory login windows measured in hours;
- hidden surprise timers for core progression;
- pay-to-avoid-loss mechanics;
- exclusive meta-defining Disciplines/Soulmarks/Confluences that late players can never obtain.

Prefer:

- unique memories;
- first-edition cosmetics;
- titles;
- Chronicle recognition;
- server history;
- special event variants;
- temporary altered rules;
- recurring rare rewards;
- competitive placement;
- social participation.

The design goal is **"I wish I had been there"**, not **"my character is permanently inferior because I had a job that week."**

## 8. World Pulse

The game shell should include a concise **World Pulse** surface that tells the player why logging in today matters.

It can show:

- active world events;
- event phase changes;
- rotating bosses/encounters;
- Expedition anomalies;
- PvP rotation/tournament windows;
- guild/nation objectives;
- limited lore discoveries currently obtainable;
- community progress;
- time remaining;
- what changed since the player's last login.

World Pulse should be personalized enough to surface relevant activities without becoming a wall of flashing countdowns.

## 9. Rekindling — AUREVANE's Prestige System

The prestige system is called **Rekindling**.

Rekindling is a voluntary second journey for a character who has completed the first 180-day minimum progression cycle and the required endgame rite.

The fantasy is not deleting the character.

The fantasy is:

> I have reached one complete version of myself. Now I choose to build again from the beginning, carrying scars, insight, reputation, and a small veteran edge into a new life-path.

This reinforces the game's theme of becoming while preserving identity and history.

## 10. What Rekindling Resets

The exact reset package is configurable, but the intended default is meaningful enough that the new cycle feels like rebuilding.

A Rekindling may reset or partially reset:

- character level to a low starting point;
- current active progression band;
- selected world progression gates where replay makes sense;
- active Discipline development except protected retained memories;
- current-cycle equipment progression except permanent collectibles/cosmetics;
- cycle-specific challenge progress;
- cycle-specific endgame qualification.

The system must never silently delete irreversible purchases, cosmetics, account ownership, social identity, or historical achievements.

## 11. What Rekindling Preserves

A character should remain recognizably the same long-lived person.

Preserve:

- name and identity;
- account ownership;
- appearance unlocks;
- cosmetic collection;
- Chronicle history;
- lore archive discoveries;
- achievements;
- titles/badges;
- social relationships;
- guild history where system rules permit;
- ranked history;
- Rekindling count;
- Hall of Selves / prior-cycle snapshots;
- selected permanent veteran unlocks;
- Master Panel/support history and auditability.

## 12. Memory Carryover

Before Rekindling, the player chooses a limited **Memory Carryover** package.

Possible examples:

- retain one mastered Discipline as a remembered Legacy candidate that still requires level/world prerequisites before use;
- retain one unlocked Confluence discovery in the Codex;
- retain one Soulmark branch insight;
- retain one crafting/Expedition knowledge perk;
- retain one selected equipment appearance or heirloom shell without carrying unbalanced old stats.

The purpose is to make each new six-month cycle faster to understand and more strategically interesting without making the second cycle trivial.

Carryover power and number of retained memories are Master Panel configurable.

## 13. Rekindling Duration

Every Rekindling is itself intended to be another long-form journey.

Production default:

- minimum Rekindled-cycle endgame time: **180 days**;
- same broad Horizon framework;
- alternate goals, routes, build incentives, and event/story context so the journey does not feel like an identical replay;
- optional modest veteran catch-up inside already-unlocked bands without bypassing the 180-day final gate.

Multiple Rekindlings create long-term account identity measured in years without requiring infinite vertical stat inflation.

## 14. The Veteran Edge — PvP-Relevant Prestige Reward

Rekindling grants access to a new character layer called the **Veteran Edge**.

The Veteran Edge is intentionally designed to matter most in high-level PvP while remaining bounded.

Key rule:

**Prestige should unlock tactical options, not endlessly stack raw stats.**

A Rekindled character gains access to a set of **Edge Techniques** and may equip only **one active Edge slot** in standard competitive play, regardless of total Rekindling count unless a specific queue says otherwise.

Further Rekindlings primarily unlock more choices, cosmetic evolutions, titles, and profile prestige rather than additional simultaneously stacked combat power.

## 15. Example Edge Techniques

Exact designs must be combat-tested and data-driven. Examples of the intended power shape:

### Read the Opening

Once per battle, during the legal pre-combat/loadout-adjustment window, gain one additional permitted loadout adjustment after seeing public opponent information.

### Veteran's Reserve

Once per battle, the first time the character ends a turn below a configured resource threshold, restore a very small amount of MP at the start of the next turn.

### Settled Nerves

Once per battle, reduce the duration/effect of the first minor control condition that exceeds a configured threshold.

### Measured Step

Once per battle, after cancelling a preview before commitment, the character may alter facing without consuming the normal minor facing opportunity if the combat rules support this cleanly.

### Known Ground

Gain a small information advantage tied to map familiarity, such as revealing one public terrain/hazard interaction before the first turn, not hidden enemy information.

These examples are not final balance promises. Their purpose is to demonstrate that Veteran Edge should create tactical texture rather than a permanent +20% damage advantage.

## 16. PvP Balance Requirements for Veteran Edge

Veteran Edge is allowed in normal PvP only if it remains within strict balance targets.

Requirements:

- one standard active Edge slot;
- no uncapped stacking by Rekindling count;
- no percentage stat package that simply makes veterans numerically superior;
- every Edge has counters or situational value;
- full combat regression tests;
- matchup/win-rate analytics by Edge;
- Master Panel kill switch for each Edge;
- queue-level setting to disable, normalize, or enable Veteran Edge;
- tournament rules may normalize Edge use when competitive integrity requires it;
- matchmaking may consider Rekindling/Edge state if analytics show material distortion.

Target guideline: no single Edge should create a persistent aggregate win-rate lift large enough to become mandatory. The owner can define numeric alert thresholds in the Master Panel.

## 17. Rekindling Rewards Beyond PvP

Each Rekindling can also grant:

- evolving portrait frame;
- profile aura;
- unique title tier;
- character-card visual;
- Hall of Selves entry;
- special emote or banner;
- access to veteran-only cosmetic challenges;
- lore-reactive NPC dialogue;
- optional advanced training/mentorship systems;
- additional Edge Technique choices;
- Chronicle distinction.

The strongest long-term reward should be identity and flexibility, not infinite vertical stats.

## 18. Hall of Selves

The profile should eventually include a **Hall of Selves** showing prior completed cycles.

Each entry can preserve:

- final level;
- final Current + Legacy pairing;
- Soulmark;
- notable Confluences;
- signature equipment appearance;
- highest PvP rank;
- major Expedition clears;
- world-event achievements;
- lore milestones;
- date Rekindled;
- selected portrait/card art.

This turns starting over into accumulating history rather than erasing history.

## 19. Lore Discovery Is a Core Progression Loop

AUREVANE's lore should not be delivered only by main-story dialogue.

Players should **find, compare, interpret, and piece together the world** through exploration.

Lore sources include:

- books;
- letters;
- diaries;
- military reports;
- temple records;
- legal decrees;
- expedition journals;
- field notes;
- inscriptions;
- murals;
- monuments;
- grave markers;
- item descriptions;
- relics;
- maps;
- NPC testimony;
- contradictory oral traditions;
- hidden rooms;
- environmental storytelling;
- boss arenas;
- recovered audio/vision equivalents where setting-appropriate;
- event aftermath;
- World Chronicle entries;
- Great Vane sites;
- Closed Star archives;
- Unchosen anomalies.

A player who loves lore should be able to spend significant time becoming an in-world historian.

## 20. The Archive

The player-facing lore collection is the **Archive**.

Archive entries should distinguish:

- **Primary Sources** — documents/objects from the era being studied;
- **Testimony** — NPC claims and oral history;
- **Institutional Records** — official histories that may contain bias or deliberate omission;
- **Field Observations** — player-character discoveries;
- **Reconstruction** — conclusions unlocked after enough evidence has been collected;
- **Unresolved Contradictions** — evidence that cannot yet be reconciled.

The Archive should not automatically declare every source true.

That is important for the Aurevane mystery.

## 21. Fragment Sets and Reconstructions

Major lore questions are represented as **Fragment Sets**.

Example:

```text
THE NAME BENEATH THE HORIZON

[✓] Weathered pilgrimage tablet
[✓] Dawnbound hymn fragment
[ ] Closed Star confiscation order
[✓] mural transcription
[ ] Expedition memory-echo

3/5 known sources
Current reconstruction:
"A forgotten divine figure was associated with second beginnings."
```

As more evidence is found, the interpretation changes.

The game can therefore reveal Aurevane through accumulation rather than a lore-dump cutscene.

## 22. Contradictory Lore

Some sources should intentionally disagree because:

- cultures remember events differently;
- states rewrote history;
- the Closed Star deliberately suppressed dangerous knowledge;
- witnesses lied;
- memory was damaged by Unchosen phenomena;
- different historical records are both partially true because reality itself was once unstable.

The Archive should preserve source provenance so players can reason about contradictions.

Do not use contradiction as an excuse for arbitrary retcons. Canon remains controlled internally through `docs/LORE_BIBLE.md`.

## 23. Lore Rewards

Lore hunting may reward:

- Archive completion;
- titles;
- cosmetics;
- map annotations;
- hidden quests;
- optional dialogue;
- Expedition branches;
- alternate boss introductions;
- safe shortcuts/knowledge advantages;
- Chronicle recognition;
- access to deeper reconstruction pages.

Do not make critical combat power depend on reading hundreds of documents.

## 24. Community Lore Discovery

Selected mysteries can be server-wide or seasonal puzzles.

Examples:

- players collectively assemble fragments from different regions;
- guilds race to decode a cipher;
- first discoverers are recorded in the Chronicle;
- a hidden event phase activates once the community reconstructs enough evidence;
- a world map location appears only after a global discovery threshold.

This produces strong social urgency/FOMO while making lore materially part of the living world.

## 25. Spoiler-Safe Lore Publication

Every lore fragment/content object should be able to carry internal metadata such as:

- canon ID;
- source type;
- era;
- region;
- story arc;
- reveal tier;
- spoiler tier;
- prerequisites;
- availability window;
- event linkage;
- confidence/reliability tag for staff use;
- whether it is repeatable, missable, archived, or permanently available;
- fallback/recurrence plan if it contains important gameplay information.

Player-facing text must never expose internal spoiler labels.

## 26. Progression Analytics

The game must measure whether six-month pacing is actually working.

Track, at minimum:

- median calendar days to levels 20/40/60/80/90/100;
- median active playtime to each milestone;
- Horizon unlock timing;
- story milestone timing;
- Disciplines mastered by character age;
- Confluences discovered by character age;
- Soulmark progression by character age;
- Expedition progression;
- PvP participation/rank by character age;
- retention/churn by progression band;
- returning-player recovery time;
- Rekindling eligibility rate;
- Rekindling completion/start rate;
- Edge Technique usage and PvP win rate;
- event participation versus event availability;
- lore-fragment discovery/completion;
- player concentration in progression bottlenecks.

The target is to tune a compelling journey, not blindly maximize session hours.

## 27. Master Panel — Progression Control Center

The Master Panel must be able to tune the progression model without code deployment.

Owner/authorized balance staff controls eventually include:

- level cap;
- XP curve by level/range;
- Mastery curve;
- Horizon Gate count/names;
- character-age requirements;
- per-Horizon level ceilings;
- milestone requirements;
- rested progression rates/caps;
- contract bank size/expiry;
- returning-player catch-up multipliers;
- world/story progression prerequisites;
- endgame rite requirements;
- first-cycle minimum age;
- Rekindling eligibility requirements;
- Rekindling minimum cycle age;
- reset/preserve rules;
- Memory Carryover count and allowed categories;
- Veteran Edge slot count;
- Edge Technique values;
- Edge availability by PvP queue;
- matchmaking weighting related to Rekindling/Edge state;
- event cadence and duration;
- event recurrence rules;
- rotating encounter schedules/weights;
- rarity and reward tables;
- season lengths;
- first-witness reward definitions;
- lore Fragment Set thresholds;
- lore availability windows;

All such changes use versioned configuration, validation, preview/simulation where practical, audit records, scheduled publication, and rollback.

## 28. Master Panel — Pacing Simulator

Before publishing major progression changes, the Balance Lab should eventually support a **Pacing Simulator**.

Inputs may include:

- XP curve;
- average session frequency;
- average activity mix;
- rested bonus assumptions;
- Horizon requirements;
- expected quest/Expedition completion;
- returning-player gaps;
- event bonuses.

Outputs should estimate:

- expected calendar days to milestone levels;
- expected active hours;
- percentage of players projected to hit gates early/late;
- likely bottlenecks;
- projected Rekindling eligibility distribution.

Simulation never replaces live telemetry, but it prevents obviously broken pacing changes from being published blindly.

## 29. Master Panel — Retention & Urgency Controls

The owner should be able to manage FOMO/urgency deliberately.

Controls include:

- event start/end;
- recurrence;
- countdown visibility;
- player eligibility;
- first-witness rewards;
- rotating boss/merchant schedules;
- limited Archive fragments;
- seasonal cosmetic availability;
- community objective thresholds;
- aftermath duration;
- notification priority;
- World Pulse placement/priority;
- catch-up/recurrence policy.

The panel should warn when a configuration would permanently strand important gameplay power behind a one-time window.

The owner may still deliberately create truly one-time prestige/cosmetic history, but competitive power should remain governed by the game's fairness rules.

## 30. Master Panel — Rekindling & Veteran Edge Controls

The owner and authorized balance staff can:

- enable/disable Rekindling globally;
- change eligibility requirements;
- alter which systems reset/persist;
- change Memory Carryover rules;
- enable/disable individual Edge Techniques;
- modify Edge coefficients/cooldowns/triggers;
- restrict Edges by queue/tournament;
- normalize all Edges for a competitive event;
- inspect Edge win/pick rates;
- simulate PvP effects;
- rollback an Edge balance package;
- grant/correct a Rekindling through an audited support action when legitimately required.

## 31. Master Panel — Lore Operations

Narrative staff with the correct permissions can:

- author Archive sources;
- place world documents/inscriptions/relics;
- build Fragment Sets;
- define reconstruction thresholds;
- set source provenance and staff-only reliability notes;
- link fragments to regions/events/quests/Expeditions;
- define spoiler/reveal tiers;
- preview the player's Archive state;
- schedule temporary lore availability;
- create aftermath/archive access;
- publish Chronicle entries;
- search which players have discovered a fragment for support/analytics purposes;
- rollback publication mistakes.

Late Aurevane revelations remain protected by higher canon/spoiler permissions as defined by the Master Panel and Lore Bible.

## 32. Owner Ultimate Control

The project Owner must ultimately have final operational authority over all game-controlled state through safe, audited tools.

This includes the ability to:

- inspect any account/character relevant to game operations;
- grant/revoke staff roles and individual permissions;
- create custom role templates;
- grant special account capabilities such as tester, event participant, tournament official, creator/partner, moderator, or internal QA access;
- grant/revoke game entitlements and approved content access;
- correct level/XP/Mastery/progression through authoritative support commands;
- correct Rekindling state;
- correct inventory/currency/rewards through domain-safe grant/reversal commands;
- grant/remove titles, cosmetics, badges, Chronicle marks, or event eligibility;
- change player story flags when support requires a repair;
- move/recover characters from broken world/session state;
- suspend/ban/restrict accounts;
- override event eligibility;
- reset a stuck quest/Expedition/battle when supported by the domain;
- change world, progression, economy, PvP, event, lore, and content configuration;
- activate feature flags and emergency kill switches;
- publish/rollback content;
- inspect audit history for every privileged change.

"Ultimate control" does not mean the browser receives raw database credentials or that normal operations become unvalidated SQL edits.

The Master Panel is the Owner's command surface over authoritative domain services.

For exceptional emergencies, a future **Break-Glass Owner Action** may expose narrowly defined high-risk operations unavailable to ordinary staff. Break-glass actions require re-authentication, explicit reason, prominent confirmation, and immutable audit records.

## 33. Owner User Modification Safety

Player modification must remain powerful and safe.

Prefer purpose-built actions such as:

- `grant_item`
- `revoke_erroneous_item`
- `adjust_currency_with_reason`
- `set_progression_milestone`
- `repair_story_flag`
- `grant_title`
- `grant_permission`
- `revoke_permission`
- `reset_quest`
- `recover_character_location`

rather than a generic browser form that can arbitrarily write every database column.

The Owner can still accomplish the required outcome, but the system preserves invariants, transactions, validation, idempotency, and auditability.

## 35. Implementation Timing

These systems are delivered progressively rather than all at once.

### Phase 0

- architecture must allow data-driven progression configuration, staff authorization, versioning, and auditing;
- no six-month timers need to block foundation development;
- document the long-term model.

### Phase 1

- implement basic level/XP progression with configuration boundaries;
- store character creation time and progression metadata required by future Horizon Gates;
- do not build full Rekindling yet.

### Phase 3–4

- integrate Discipline/Soulmark/Confluence milestones into long-term progression telemetry;
- prepare build-history snapshot structures where useful.

### Phase 5

- implement World Pulse foundations;
- Archive/lore discovery foundation;
- Fragment Sets;
- world documents/relics/inscriptions;
- event urgency/recurrence metadata;
- initial Master Panel controls for events/lore;
- first meaningful Horizon/world gates if required by the playable content.

### Phase 7–8

- use Expeditions and PvP as major mid/late-cycle progression pillars;
- add seasonal/competitive urgency;
- instrument PvP balance data required before Veteran Edge launches.

### Phase 9–12

- deepen endgame requirements with broader Discipline, guild, economy, and nation systems;
- finish the first complete six-month progression loop.

### Phase 13

- complete progression/retention/Rekindling/lore control surfaces in the Master Panel;
- Pacing Simulator;
- player correction tools;
- special permissions/entitlements;
- advanced telemetry and rollback.

### Endgame / Alpha-to-Live progression milestone

- validate that an engaged first-cycle character cannot reach full endgame/Rekindling eligibility before the configured six-month minimum;
- ensure the journey remains enjoyable rather than merely time-gated;
- launch Rekindling only after the first-cycle endgame is genuinely worth replaying;
- launch Veteran Edge only after sufficient PvP telemetry and automated regression coverage exist.

## 36. Definition of Success

This direction succeeds when:

- a serious player has meaningful goals throughout at least six months of first-cycle progression;
- the six-month requirement comes from layered character/world growth, not empty waiting;
- live events and seasons make players care about what is happening now;
- missing a window can mean missing a unique memory or prestige reward without permanently ruining competitive viability;
- reaching endgame feels like completing a personal era;
- Rekindling makes starting over exciting instead of destructive;
- veteran PvP rewards provide bounded tactical distinction rather than runaway stat inflation;
- multiple Rekindlings can support years of character identity;
- lore is discovered through the actual world, not only exposition;
- players can collectively solve mysteries and leave discoveries in server history;
- the Owner can rebalance every major pacing, retention, Rekindling, PvP Edge, lore, and user-support control from the Master Panel;
- every privileged production change remains server-authoritative, validated, versioned where appropriate, and auditable.
