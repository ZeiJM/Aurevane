# AUREVANE — Long-Horizon Progression, Rekindling & Retention

**Status:** Authoritative progression/retention specification subordinate to `docs/GAME_MASTER_PLAN.md` and `docs/GAME_MASTER_PLAN_BUILD_SYSTEM_ADDENDUM.md`.

**Initial direction approved:** 2026-08-15.  
**Build/progression terminology synchronized:** 2026-08-19.

This document defines AUREVANE's minimum six-month first-character journey, endgame pacing, healthy urgency/FOMO systems, repeatable prestige progression, PvP-safe veteran advantages, lore-discovery loop, and the Master Panel controls required to operate and rebalance those systems without routine code changes.

Current build terminology applies throughout:

- Primary Discipline;
- optional mastered Secondary Discipline;
- Discipline Skills;
- Resonance for mixed builds;
- Essence for pure builds;
- Soulmark or Soul-Severed/Mantle supernatural path.

Legacy/Confluence/Art-era terminology is retired from current progression requirements.

---

## 1. Product Goal

AUREVANE should feel like a world a player inhabits for months, not a game solved in a weekend.

The first serious character journey from creation to first full endgame/Rekindling readiness should take **at least approximately six calendar months** for a highly engaged player under production defaults.

The target is not six months of repetitive grinding or empty waiting. It is six months of:

- character levels;
- Discipline Mastery;
- Primary/Secondary experimentation;
- mixed Resonance and pure Essence build development;
- Soulmark or Soul-Severed/Mantle progression;
- equipment refinement;
- world/story progression;
- lore discovery;
- Expeditions;
- PvP seasons;
- guild/event participation;
- preparation for the first Rekindling.

The character should regularly change in meaningful ways while still having important horizons ahead.

---

## 2. What “Six Months Minimum” Means

The first full journey is measured from character creation to **First Horizon / Rekindling eligibility**, not merely Level 1 to an XP number.

Production default minimum character age is **180 days**.

Endgame readiness requires categories such as:

- minimum character age;
- required character level;
- required story/world milestone;
- required number of mastered Disciplines;
- meaningful build-development milestones appropriate to the current Primary/Secondary/Resonance/Essence system;
- supernatural-path progression where required;
- endgame PvE qualification such as Standard/Deep Expedition progress;
- an endgame rite/challenge proving the player can use the character rather than merely wait out time.

Time alone is insufficient. Grind alone is insufficient.

---

## 3. Level 1–100 Pacing

Level 1–100 remains.

Broad target envelope:

```text
Weeks 1–2       Levels 1–20
Weeks 3–6       Levels 21–40
Months 2–3      Levels 41–60
Months 3–4      Levels 61–75
Months 4–5      Levels 76–90
Months 5–6+     Levels 91–100 / first-cycle endgame
```

This is pacing guidance, not an exact promise.

Skilled/engaged players can progress more efficiently, master more Disciplines, experiment more deeply and clear harder content sooner, but should not bypass the long-form journey in a few days.

---

## 4. Horizon Gates

AUREVANE avoids mandatory daily energy and crude dead-stop stamina gating.

Use **Horizon Gates**: broad character-age/progression bands controlling when later progression tiers can open.

Example production defaults:

```text
HORIZON I      Day 0+       early world / Foundation growth
HORIZON II     Day 21+      intermediate progression
HORIZON III    Day 45+      advanced systems deepen
HORIZON IV     Day 75+      high-level regions/content
HORIZON V      Day 110+     veteran content
HORIZON VI     Day 150+     pre-endgame
FIRST HORIZON  Day 180+     Level-100/endgame rite + Rekindling eligibility
```

Each Horizon also requires gameplay milestones. A dormant six-month-old character does not automatically become endgame-ready.

Exact names, dates, thresholds, milestones and enabled state are data-driven and Master Panel configurable.

---

## 5. Catch-Up & Absence Protection

Use healthy absence protection such as:

- explicit Passive Training;
- future rested progression where approved;
- banked contracts;
- accelerated catch-up inside already-unlocked older bands;
- returning-player objectives;
- catch-up bundles that help resume rather than leapfrog the journey;
- recurring access to important build components.

A player missing time should feel behind on current happenings, not permanently ruined.

Passive Training follows `docs/OFFLINE_PROGRESSION.md`: current sessions are explicit Short/Medium/Extended server-timed plans, not automatic idle accrual.

---

## 6. Healthy Urgency / Experiential FOMO

AUREVANE deliberately creates:

> “Something is happening in the world and I want to be there.”

Good urgency comes from world life, social history, discovery, prestige and cosmetics—not punitive account damage.

Core systems can include:

### Live world events

Time-bounded changes to regions, encounters, NPCs, bosses, story state, music and community objectives.

### First-witness recognition

Possible rewards:

- Chronicle witness marks;
- badges/titles;
- commemorative cosmetics;
- portrait frames;
- guild/server-history recognition.

These are prestige/social rewards, not permanent meta-defining power.

### Rotating encounters/content

Temporary elites, merchants, anomalies, Expedition modifiers, lore fragments or event variants may appear. Important build-enabling rewards recur or have alternate paths.

### Seasons

PvP/world/guild/Expedition/cosmetic rotations create distinct periods.

### Community races

Guild/server races can involve lore puzzles, new bosses, event stabilization, Expedition discovery or Chronicle milestones.

### Event aftermath

Missed events leave evidence through NPC dialogue, Chronicle history, altered locations, aftermath quests and archived documents.

---

## 7. FOMO Guardrails

Do not make the primary urgency system:

- permanent combat power that never returns;
- destructive login streaks;
- mandatory hour-scale login windows;
- hidden surprise timers for core progression;
- pay-to-avoid-loss mechanics;
- permanently exclusive meta-defining Disciplines, Resonances, Essence Skills, Soulmarks, Mantles or equipment.

Prefer memorable presence, cosmetics, titles, history, variants, temporary rule changes, recurring rare rewards, competitive placement and social participation.

Target feeling:

> “I wish I had been there.”

not:

> “My character is permanently inferior because life got busy.”

---

## 8. World Pulse

A concise **World Pulse** tells players why today matters.

It may surface:

- active events;
- phase changes;
- rotating bosses/encounters;
- Expedition anomalies;
- PvP/tournament windows;
- guild/nation objectives;
- time-limited lore opportunities;
- community progress;
- what changed since last visit;
- completed Passive Training/return context where useful.

It should be relevant without becoming a wall of flashing timers.

---

## 9. Rekindling

**Rekindling** is the prestige system.

A character who completes a full minimum journey and required endgame rite may voluntarily begin another meaningful long-form cycle while remaining the same long-lived identity.

The fantasy is:

> “I reached one complete version of myself. Now I choose to build again, carrying history, insight and a bounded veteran edge into another path.”

Rekindling is not character deletion.

---

## 10. What Rekindling May Reset

Exact package is configurable, but the new cycle must feel like rebuilding.

It may reset/partially reset:

- level to a low starting point;
- active progression band;
- selected replayable world gates;
- current-cycle Discipline development except approved retained memories;
- current-cycle equipment progression except protected collectibles/cosmetics;
- cycle-specific challenges;
- cycle-specific endgame qualification.

Never silently delete irreversible purchases, cosmetics, account ownership, identity, social history or permanent achievements.

The Soulmarked versus Soul-Severed supernatural choice **persists across Rekindling** unless a future explicit story system says otherwise.

---

## 11. What Rekindling Preserves

Preserve identity/history such as:

- name/account identity;
- appearance unlocks/cosmetics;
- Chronicle history;
- Archive discoveries;
- achievements/titles/badges;
- social relationships;
- guild/ranked history where rules permit;
- Rekindling count;
- Hall of Selves snapshots;
- approved permanent veteran unlocks;
- supernatural-path identity;
- support/audit history.

---

## 12. Memory Carryover

Before Rekindling, a player may choose a limited **Memory Carryover** package.

Possible examples under the current build model:

- retain one Discipline Mastery memory/shortcut under bounded prerequisites;
- retain one known Resonance discovery in the Codex/Archive knowledge layer;
- retain pure-Discipline Essence knowledge where the system supports persistent discovery;
- retain one Soulmark branch insight or Mantle-path knowledge consistent with the character's permanent supernatural choice;
- retain one crafting/Expedition knowledge perk;
- retain one equipment appearance/heirloom shell without carrying unbalanced stats.

Carryover should make later cycles more strategically interesting, not trivial.

---

## 13. Rekindling Duration

Each Rekindled cycle remains long-form.

Production default:

- minimum roughly 180 days to another full endgame/Rekindling eligibility;
- same broad Horizon philosophy;
- alternate routes/goals/build incentives/story context;
- modest veteran catch-up within already-unlocked bands without bypassing the final gate.

Multiple Rekindlings build years of identity without infinite vertical stat inflation.

---

## 14. Veteran Edge

Rekindling unlocks access to **Veteran Edge** choices.

Core rule:

> **Prestige unlocks bounded tactical options, not endlessly stacked raw stats.**

Standard competitive play uses only **one active Veteran Edge slot** where the system is enabled, regardless of Rekindling count unless a specific mode says otherwise.

Further cycles primarily broaden choices, cosmetics, titles and prestige rather than stack simultaneous combat power.

---

## 15. Veteran Edge Example Shape

Examples are design-shape references, not final balance promises:

### Read the Opening

Once per battle, gain one additional permitted pre-combat/loadout adjustment after seeing public opponent information where the mode supports such a window.

### Veteran's Reserve

Once per battle, after ending below a configured resource threshold, restore a very small bounded amount next turn.

### Settled Nerves

Once per battle, reduce the first eligible minor control effect under explicit rules.

### Known Ground

Provide a small public-information advantage tied to map familiarity, never hidden enemy information.

Veteran Edge must create tactical texture, not permanent percentage supremacy.

---

## 16. PvP Veteran Edge Requirements

- one standard active Edge slot;
- no uncapped stacking;
- no blanket percentage stat package;
- situational value/counterplay;
- combat regressions;
- analytics by Edge;
- Master Panel kill switch;
- queue-level enable/disable/normalize controls;
- tournament normalization where required;
- matchmaking consideration only if telemetry proves material distortion.

No Edge should become mandatory for competitive viability.

---

## 17. Rekindling Rewards Beyond Combat

Possible rewards:

- evolving portrait frame/profile aura;
- title tiers;
- character-card presentation;
- Hall of Selves entry;
- emotes/banners;
- veteran cosmetic challenges;
- lore-reactive dialogue;
- advanced mentorship/training systems;
- additional Edge choices;
- Chronicle distinction.

Identity/flexibility should outweigh raw power.

---

## 18. Hall of Selves

Hall of Selves can preserve prior-cycle snapshots such as:

- final level;
- final Primary Discipline;
- optional Secondary Discipline;
- pure Essence or mixed Resonance route;
- supernatural identity;
- signature equipment appearance;
- highest PvP rank;
- Expedition clears;
- world-event achievements;
- lore milestones;
- Rekindling date;
- portrait/card art.

Starting over accumulates history rather than erasing it.

---

## 19. Lore Discovery Is Progression

Players should find, compare and reconstruct lore through:

- books/letters/diaries;
- military/temple/legal records;
- Expedition journals/field notes;
- inscriptions/murals/monuments/graves;
- item descriptions/relics/maps;
- NPC testimony/oral tradition;
- hidden rooms/environmental storytelling;
- boss arenas;
- event aftermath;
- Chronicle;
- Great Vane/Closed Star/Unmoored evidence where progression permits.

Lore-focused players should be able to become in-world historians.

---

## 20. Archive

Archive categories can include:

- **Primary Sources**;
- **Testimony**;
- **Institutional Records**;
- **Field Observations**;
- **Reconstruction**;
- **Unresolved Contradictions**.

The Archive preserves provenance and does not automatically declare every source true.

That is essential to the central mystery.

---

## 21. Fragment Sets & Reconstructions

Major questions can use Fragment Sets.

Example:

```text
THE NAME BENEATH THE HORIZON

[✓] Weathered pilgrimage tablet
[✓] Dawnbound hymn fragment
[ ] Closed Star confiscation order
[✓] mural transcription
[ ] Expedition memory-echo

3/5 known sources
Current reconstruction:
“A forgotten divine figure was associated with second beginnings.”
```

Interpretation evolves as evidence accumulates.

---

## 22. Contradictory Lore

Sources may disagree because cultures remember differently, institutions rewrote history, witnesses lied, memory was damaged, or reality itself was unstable.

The system preserves source identity and uncertainty rather than flattening all lore into one omniscient encyclopedia.

Do not use the Archive to spoil late reveals simply because developers know the truth.

---

## 23. Archive Rewards & Competition

Lore progression can reward:

- knowledge;
- Archive completion;
- titles/badges;
- cosmetics;
- access to optional investigations/quests;
- Chronicle recognition;
- social discovery prestige.

Core competitive combat power should not require obscure one-time lore fragments that never recur.

---

## 24. Relationship to Build Discovery

Build discovery remains a primary retention pillar.

Players should have reasons to ask:

- Which Discipline do I want as Primary?
- Which mastered Discipline should I equip as Secondary?
- What Resonance does the pairing unlock?
- What changes if I reverse Primary/Secondary and therefore change the base Discipline profile/Skill emphasis?
- Is this build stronger or more interesting if I stay pure and use its Essence Skill?
- How does my Soulmark—or Mantle path on a Soul-Severed character—change the plan?
- What equipment/Equipment Skill makes this strategy work?

This replaces the old Legacy/Confluence framing without changing the underlying theorycraft pillar.

---

## 25. Retention Without Misery

Primary healthy retention systems include:

- Mastery;
- build discovery;
- Resonance/Essence exploration;
- targeted equipment hunting;
- Expeditions;
- PvP seasons;
- guild/social play;
- world events;
- new Disciplines/supernatural content;
- Passive Training/return support;
- contract banking;
- Archive/lore discovery;
- Rekindling/prestige identity.

Avoid destructive streaks, mandatory daily energy, dozens of currencies, pay-to-avoid-loss and permanent build traps.

---

## 26. Master Panel Controls

Owner/delegated systems eventually control:

- Horizon configuration;
- XP/Mastery pacing;
- Passive Training and catch-up parameters;
- endgame/Rekindling requirements;
- Memory Carryover rules;
- Veteran Edge content/queue legality;
- urgency/event schedules;
- returning-player assistance;
- Archive/Fragment Set publication;
- build-milestone requirements using current Primary/Secondary/Resonance/Essence terminology;
- telemetry thresholds and alerts.

All changes are versioned/audited/rollback-capable.

---

## 27. Pacing Simulator

Pacing simulation should model player profiles such as:

- highly engaged daily player;
- moderate several-times-per-week player;
- weekend-heavy player;
- returning player after absence;
- PvE-heavy/PvP-heavy/social-heavy mixes.

Outputs can estimate:

- level/Horizon timing;
- Discipline Mastery timing;
- Primary/Secondary build maturity;
- Passive Training contribution;
- Expedition/PvP access timing;
- first Rekindling eligibility;
- later-cycle pacing.

Simulation informs humans; it never automatically rebalance live progression.

---

## 28. Analytics

Track whether progression is healthy, including:

- time to levels/Horizons;
- Mastery stages;
- Primary/Secondary pairing rates;
- pure vs mixed build usage;
- Resonance usage/performance;
- Essence usage/performance;
- supernatural-path usage;
- equipment acquisition/use;
- Passive Training contribution;
- event participation;
- Archive discovery;
- Expedition/PvP progression;
- churn/return by progression band;
- Rekindling eligibility and completion;
- Veteran Edge usage/performance.

Analytics must distinguish correlation from causation; humans approve tuning.

---

## 29. Implementation Timing

### Early phases

Build only the progression foundations required by current gameplay:

- Character XP/levels;
- six attributes;
- Discipline Mastery foundation;
- Primary foundation;
- current Passive Training;
- telemetry/config boundaries.

### Phase 3 — Signature Buildcraft

Introduce:

- mastered Secondary Discipline;
- Primary/Secondary attunement rules;
- mixed 6-Skill and pure 8-Skill capacities;
- Resonance;
- pure Essence;
- required build-development telemetry.

### Phase 5 — Living World

Add World Pulse, Horizon/world gates, Archive/lore systems, event aftermath and richer return loops.

### Later phases

Add the PvE/PvP/social/economy/nation systems required to make the long journey varied rather than repetitive.

### Phase 13 — Master Panel

Complete pacing, progression, Rekindling, Veteran Edge, Archive and retention operations/analytics.

### Phase 15 — Hardening

Validate:

- 180-day gate integrity;
- no accidental shortcut through Passive Training or grind;
- server-time authority;
- Rekindling preserve/reset integrity;
- supernatural-choice persistence;
- Veteran Edge competitive bounds;
- event recurrence/catch-up;
- analytics accuracy;
- Owner controls/audit/rollback.

---

## 30. Definition of Success

The progression/retention system succeeds when:

- a character feels meaningfully different throughout a months-long journey;
- highly engaged players always have interesting goals without completing the entire character arc in days;
- absence is recoverable rather than catastrophic;
- Passive Training helps without replacing play;
- Primary/Secondary/Resonance/Essence experimentation remains compelling;
- Soulmark vs Soul-Severed/Mantle identity matters without becoming pay-to-win;
- world events create desirable presence without permanent competitive ruin;
- Archive/lore discovery becomes a real hobby;
- Rekindling adds history/flexibility rather than erasing identity;
- Veteran Edge stays bounded and competitively manageable;
- the Owner can tune the system through versioned operations rather than code edits;
- human behavior/telemetry supports the intended six-month pacing instead of hidden coercive timers.
