# AUREVANE — External Identity Consultant Brief

**Status:** Non-canonical development brief.  
**Purpose:** Give a secondary external consultant enough context to challenge AUREVANE's originality, identify generic fantasy drift, and propose improvements without overwriting protected canon or current mechanics.

This file is not authority. External proposals must be canon-gated against the repository before adoption.

---

# Prompt for Claude / secondary consultant

You are acting as a **secondary senior creative director, RPG worldbuilding consultant, narrative systems designer, and originality critic** for an in-development game called **AUREVANE**.

Your job is **not** to rewrite the project from scratch. Your job is to aggressively identify where it risks feeling like generic fantasy and propose stronger, more specific alternatives that preserve the existing core.

Assume another lead designer is already responsible for final decisions. You are the adversarial second opinion.

## The game

AUREVANE is an original persistent browser-based multiplayer tactical fantasy RPG with deep buildcraft, tactical combat, a living world, PvP, co-op, Expeditions, long-term progression, and a strong narrative about identity, becoming, choice, limitation, memory, history, and consequence.

The central thematic question is:

> **What does it mean to become someone new without ceasing to be yourself?**

The player fantasy is:

> I can grow, change, learn, master new things, choose another path, and still remain me.

The game's mythological extreme is Aurevane, an ancient imprisoned goddess of Becoming, possibility, transformation, chosen identity, roads not taken, reinvention, thresholds, dawn, and second beginnings.

She was genuinely compassionate and historically liberating. She opposed inherited roles, rigid social destiny, and needless suffering.

Her fatal conclusion became:

> If becoming is good, why should any version of the self ever have to be discarded? Why should any road remain untaken? Why should death, mistakes, limitation, unwanted identity, or one chosen history ever be final?

She discovered **the Unchosen**: unrealized possibilities exerting metaphysical pressure around reality. They are **not** a casual multiverse.

Her attempts to actualize incompatible possibilities produced continuity failures: contradictory evidence, incompatible histories, people encountering unrealized selves, maps and roads disagreeing, and eventually the **City That Was Twice**.

Her final doctrine, the **Great Opening**, argued that suffering exists because reality demands exclusion. Her intended liberation would have destroyed coherent identity, consequence, history, and reality.

The resulting **War of the Last Horizon** was morally mixed. Aurevane had sincere supporters for good reasons; her opponents were not universally righteous.

Aurevane could not simply be killed, so her enemies created the **Closed Horizon**, a prison and metaphysical constraint built around limitation voluntarily accepted. It preserves the principle that one reality must remain meaningfully real.

The Binding includes **Eight Great Vanes** distributed through the known world and a protected late-story **Heart-Lock**. The Vanes are not eight generic collectible crystals.

The world contains an unstable frontier called the **Unwritten Reach**, where the world's agreement with itself begins to fail. Stable locations called **Anchors** remain while **Driftspace** between them can resolve differently. Old incorrect maps can become evidence. A modern **Inward Drift** is causing Reach-like failures inside previously stable lands. Ordinary people increasingly whisper:

> **“The Edge is closing in.”**

The Veyr are continuity-adapted peoples shaped by the outer wounds and aftermath of the ancient war. They are not an evil race, one homogeneous culture, Aurevane's minions, or omniscient truth-keepers.

## Character identity and gameplay

Characters learn combat **Disciplines**.

A mature build can have:

- one Primary Discipline;
- an optional mastered Secondary Discipline;
- Discipline Skills;
- either mixed-build **Resonance** or pure-build **Essence**;
- equipment and Equipment Skills;
- bounded long-term prestige.

Rekindling is the long-term prestige system, but it keeps the **same character**. Later cycles should differ through remembered history, Memory Carryover, Echo Routes, altered NPC relationships, and a **Hall of Selves**.

Hall of Selves principle:

> **These are not every person I could have been. These are the people I truly chose to become.**

The game's permanent character identity layer is called **Soul State**, not “supernatural path.”

Ordinary Soul States are:

- Unawakened;
- Soulmarked;
- Soul-Severed.

Soulmarks are potentials made more visible because the ancient Binding altered the metaphysical structure of the world. They are **not shards or pieces of Aurevane**.

The Severance is an irreversible choice that makes a person Soul-Severed, permanently closing ordinary Soulmark access and allowing pursuit of Mantles. Exact Severance metaphysics and final six Mantle identities are still awaiting a dedicated lore pass.

## The eight regions

They are large regions, not eight monoculture nations.

- **Aureth Crown** — central civilization; witness, provenance, oath, civic memory; tension between continuity protecting people vs protecting power.
- **Verdant Expanse** — forests, beasts, ancient ruins; growth, stewardship, inherited living memory; roots that can nourish or constrain.
- **Emberreach** — volcanic frontier; visible consequence, transformation, repair, ambition, acknowledged cost; not simply forge culture.
- **Frostmere** — frozen mountains; continuity of custody, preservation, reliability; not a cartoon gerontocracy.
- **Glasswind Desert** — mirage country and buried civilization; tested certainty and disciplined doubt; not a mystical trader stereotype.
- **Hollow Coast** — haunted shoreline; memory, grief, endings, finality; not nihilism or generic undead coast.
- **Starfall Highlands** — astral anomalies, observation, foresight, distance, and dragons; not an astrologer nation or universal dragon riders.
- **Umbral March** — dangerous high-level frontier; honest uncertainty and unresolved evidence; not simply a purple shadow biome.

## Current concern

The project has a distinctive philosophical/cosmological core, but familiar fantasy scaffolding could still make it look generic at the surface level.

Examples of risks:

- fire region / ice region / forest region / desert region;
- generic fantasy creatures;
- ruins that are only “ancient magical ruins”;
- NPCs as quest dispensers;
- cultural stereotypes;
- generic elemental color coding;
- fantasy naming that substitutes weird spelling for real identity;
- quests that could be copied into any RPG;
- lore ideas that are strong on paper but do not affect player experience.

## Your assignment

Give a rigorous consultant review answering all of the following.

### 1. Originality diagnosis

Identify the **10 biggest ways AUREVANE could still feel generic** despite its strong core.

For each, explain:

- why it is a risk;
- what a weaker fantasy game would do;
- what AUREVANE should do instead.

### 2. Protect the strongest identity

Identify the **10 strongest existing ideas that should not be diluted or casually rewritten**.

Explain why each is distinctive and how to express it in actual gameplay/world content rather than only lore documents.

### 3. Regional identity

For all eight regions, propose ways to make them recognizable without relying on biome, palette, or one cultural stereotype.

For each region provide:

- material culture;
- ordinary daily-life detail;
- an internal political/social disagreement;
- a quest shape;
- a creature/ecology direction;
- a ruin/history direction;
- a visual motif;
- an audio/ambience idea;
- one cliché to explicitly avoid.

Do **not** convert the regions into monocultures or eight obvious Great-Vane societies.

### 4. Bestiary identity

Design a **creature-authoring philosophy** that supports:

- ordinary ecology;
- creatures culturally entangled with people;
- creatures shaped by history;
- a rarer category of continuity-affected creatures.

Propose 8–12 example creature concepts as **seeds**, not mandatory canon.

Continuity creatures must have clear rules rather than random “glitch monster” behavior.

### 5. Ruins and archaeology

Propose a framework that makes ruins feel historically layered.

Include 5 example site concepts, at least:

- two completely ordinary historical sites;
- one site with disputed interpretation but no supernatural explanation;
- one site with genuine contradictory evidence;
- one dangerous late-story continuity site.

Do not make every ruin connected to Aurevane.

### 6. Quest identity

Propose 10 quest concepts that could only naturally belong in AUREVANE.

Important: not all should be morally grey, not all should be about the goddess, and not all should involve continuity phenomena.

Favor persistent consequences and relationships over fake dialogue-choice complexity.

### 7. Recurring NPCs

Propose a functional ensemble of recurring NPC archetypes who can grow with the player over multiple phases/cycles.

Do not finalize proper names unless absolutely necessary.

Avoid making everyone a secret immortal, chosen one, cultist, or exposition machine.

### 8. Soul States

Recommend how Soulmarked and Soul-Severed should matter culturally and socially without becoming a simple “magic faction A vs B” divide.

Preserve:

- Soul State terminology;
- permanence;
- Soulmark origin from Binding-altered metaphysics;
- no ordinary Soulmark/Mantle coexistence;
- Rekindling persistence.

### 9. Aurevane herself

Explain how to make Aurevane genuinely persuasive and emotionally dangerous without making her secretly correct or obviously evil.

Give examples of situations where she is **correct about an injustice but wrong about the conclusion she draws from it**.

### 10. Visual and audio identity

Propose a coherent visual and sonic vocabulary around:

- horizons;
- roads;
- thresholds;
- continuity;
- visible repair/history;
- possibility;
- exclusion;
- remembered identity.

Avoid over-branding every asset with the same symbol.

### 11. Anti-overcorrection

Identify ways the project could become **too self-consciously unique**, exhausting, pretentious, over-philosophical, or difficult to understand.

Recommend guardrails.

### 12. Prioritized recommendation

Finish with:

- **5 changes to do immediately in design documentation**;
- **5 things to address during the first world-content production phase**;
- **5 things to postpone** until later because defining them now would reduce mystery or create unnecessary scope.

## Hard constraints

Do not replace these core truths:

- Aurevane is the goddess, not the world name.
- The Unchosen are unrealized possibilities exerting pressure, not a generic multiverse.
- City That Was Twice remains foundational.
- Closed Horizon preserves one meaningful reality through limitation.
- Great Vanes are not eight collectible crystals.
- Heart-Lock remains protected and unresolved.
- Soulmark origin is Binding-related and Soulmarks are not pieces of Aurevane.
- Soul State is the umbrella term.
- Rekindling keeps the same character.
- Hall of Selves records chosen lived selves, not every hypothetical self.
- Veyr remain internally diverse and incomplete in their knowledge.
- regions are not monocultures.
- not everything is secretly about Aurevane.

Be willing to reject weak ideas.

Do not flatter the project.

The goal is to make the final game **specific, memorable, emotionally coherent, mechanically expressive, and unmistakably AUREVANE** rather than merely “original lore on top of a familiar RPG.”

# How to evaluate the response

The lead designer should accept an external proposal only if it:

1. preserves protected canon;
2. makes the underlying idea stronger without requiring novelty for novelty's sake;
3. can eventually affect player experience, not only prose;
4. preserves internal diversity;
5. does not make every system about the central mystery;
6. respects production scope and roadmap timing;
7. can be explained clearly to players;
8. strengthens AUREVANE even after all proper nouns are removed.

External suggestions remain proposals until explicitly accepted into authoritative documents.
