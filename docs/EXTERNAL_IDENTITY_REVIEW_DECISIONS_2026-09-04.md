# AUREVANE — External Identity Review Decisions (2026-09-04)

**Status:** NON-CANONICAL development/provenance record.  
**Purpose:** Preserve the outcome of the external adversarial originality review so future work does not depend on chat transcripts.

This file records what was accepted, reworked, rejected, or deferred from the external originality/worldbuilding review performed after the initial AUREVANE identity hardening pass.

Approved outcomes have been translated into authoritative direction in `docs/AUREVANE_IDENTITY_CONSULTANT_SYNTHESIS.md` and related identity/lore documents. This file does not independently create canon.

---

# 1. Review quality assessment

The external review was valuable because it correctly identified several generic-fantasy risks already being targeted internally:

- element/biome palette coding;
- collectible-MacGuffin handling of the Eight Great Vanes;
- Veyr monoculture risk;
- quest-dispenser NPCs;
- generic ancient-magical ruins;
- Soul State becoming faction A versus faction B;
- Aurevane becoming obviously evil or secretly correct;
- Disciplines reading as renamed classes;
- generic visual glitch treatment for continuity phenomena;
- artificial fantasy naming.

The review also supplied useful practical proposals for regional material culture, archaeology confidence, recurring NPC roles, quest tone variety, visual/audio registers, and mechanical clarity.

Its weakest areas were suggestions that crossed from creative texture into unsupported metaphysical fact or premature roadmap changes.

# 2. Accepted strongly

The following directions were adopted substantially:

## 2.1 Correct diagnosis → catastrophic universalization

Aurevane should often correctly identify a specific injustice, hypocrisy, grief, imposed identity, or abusive limitation before making the illegitimate leap that limitation/exclusion/finality itself is the universal problem.

This is now an explicit scene-design diagnostic.

## 2.2 Archaeology confidence model

Distinguish:

- CERTAIN;
- PROBABLE;
- DISPUTED;
- CONTRADICTORY.

This protects ordinary historical uncertainty from being confused with genuine continuity failure.

## 2.3 Mechanical clarity does not require lore literacy

Players must be able to understand tactical/system behavior without first understanding metaphysics.

Lore deepens meaning; it does not gate basic comprehension.

## 2.4 Ordinary/low-stakes recurring characters

At least some recurring NPCs should remain intentionally ordinary and personally memorable without becoming secret plot devices.

## 2.5 Ambient dialogue restraint

Routine NPC barks should sound like people living their lives, not philosophy textbooks.

## 2.6 Regional sound as livelihood

Regional audio should reveal work, infrastructure, ritual timing and daily practices rather than climate alone.

## 2.7 City That Was Twice should eventually be experiential

The City should be encountered through material contradiction and exploration, not only explained in exposition.

This is a long-term story goal, not authorization to implement it early.

## 2.8 Hall of Selves records lived identities

Hall entries must represent actually committed/lived character histories, not hypothetical loadout previews.

## 2.9 Bestiary categories

Maintain a healthy mix of:

- ordinary ecology;
- culturally entangled creatures;
- historically shaped creatures;
- rare continuity-affected creatures.

## 2.10 Region naming should have systems, not random fantasy syllables

Lightweight phonetic/historical naming sheets should precede mass proper-noun production.

# 3. Accepted with modification

## 3.1 "Vanes as load-bearing infrastructure"

Useful intention: Vanes should matter materially to civilization and not feel like collectibles.

Problem: communities knowingly operating Vane infrastructure would reveal too much and undermine existing hidden-Vane direction.

Final treatment:

- communities can depend on institutions, routes, sites, observatories, archives, customs or structures later discovered to have Vane relationships;
- most people do not know the true metaphysical reason;
- Great Vanes stay hidden inside believable history.

## 3.2 "Over-consistent continuity creatures"

Useful and distinctive as one creature grammar.

Problem: making it the universal continuity-creature rule would merely replace "glitch monster" with another rigid gimmick.

Final treatment:

- no generic glitch aesthetic;
- all continuity creatures need bounded/evidenced rules;
- over-consistency is an approved subtype, not the only subtype.

## 3.3 Soul State as social/legal status

Useful intention: Soul State should affect society beyond combat.

Problem: comparison to a medical/legal status is too narrow if treated literally, and proposed blanket civic incapacities invented unsupported metaphysics.

Final treatment:

- Soul States may influence privacy, law, employment, religion, family expectations and prejudice;
- no universal faction split;
- no universal legal incapacity;
- specific restrictions require culture/history/evidence and may be unjust or mistaken.

## 3.4 Regional naming customs

Useful as local seeds.

Problem: making every region use one naming gimmick recreates monoculture at the language level.

Final treatment:

- regional/cultural naming families need internal variation;
- examples such as grafted names, witness-linked titles or funerary renaming remain local possibilities unless separately canonized.

## 3.5 2–3 recurring NPCs per region before one-off content

Useful emphasis on recurring cast.

Problem: 16–24 fully developed NPCs before world-content production is unnecessary front-loaded scope.

Final treatment:

- establish cross-region scaffolding;
- then prove small recurring ensembles through regional vertical slices.

## 3.6 Non-Aurevane content quota

Useful intention: stop cosmic over-centralization.

Problem: fixed numeric quotas create mechanical authoring behavior and fail at different story tiers.

Final treatment:

- retain `60/30/10` as a non-binding instinct;
- review mystery concentration contextually.

## 3.7 Disciplines as philosophies of action

Useful intention: prevent class reskins.

Final treatment:

- every Discipline needs a distinct tactical proposition and repeated player decision;
- philosophical resonance is welcome where natural;
- do not force every combat kit into overt ideology.

# 4. Rejected as canon

The following ideas are not current AUREVANE canon:

- Soul-Severed people are inherently unreliable witnesses;
- Soul-Severed people cannot swear normal oaths;
- Severance makes someone unable to participate in ordinary continuity;
- Soul State automatically determines universal civic eligibility;
- every continuity creature must follow one "over-consistency" rule;
- every culture must have one identity-themed naming gimmick;
- communities openly know they depend on a Great Vane;
- exact regional creature names such as Witness-corvid, Bank-elk, Scar-moth, Verification-hare, etc.;
- exact regional quests from the review;
- exact recurring NPC archetypes as mandatory cast;
- any hard percentage quota for non-Aurevane content.

Many of these remain useful inspiration or examples, but none should be mistaken for locked canon.

---

# 5. Deferred deliberately

The review suggested several strong long-horizon priorities that remain deferred to their proper phases:

- physical City That Was Twice implementation;
- Hall of Selves implementation;
- full Veyr political/faction map;
- broad continuity-creature roster;
- PvP framing involving Soul States;
- final regional naming conventions;
- full recurring NPC cast for all eight regions.

Their design implications are recorded now so later work can prepare intelligently without violating roadmap sequencing.

# 6. Protected concepts unchanged

The review did not alter:

- Aurevane is the goddess, not the world name;
- Unchosen are unrealized possibilities exerting pressure, not a generic multiverse;
- City That Was Twice remains foundational;
- Closed Horizon preserves one meaningful reality through voluntarily accepted limitation;
- Great Vanes are not collectible crystals;
- Heart-Lock remains protected and unresolved;
- Soulmark origin remains Binding-related;
- Soulmarks are not fragments of Aurevane;
- Soul State remains the umbrella term;
- Soulmarked and Soul-Severed remain ordinary mutually exclusive resolved states;
- normal Soulmark/Mantle coexistence remains prohibited;
- Rekindling keeps the same character;
- Hall of Selves records chosen/lived selves;
- Veyr remain diverse and incompletely informed;
- regions are not monocultures;
- not everything is secretly about Aurevane;
- final Severance metaphysics and six Mantle identities remain for a dedicated later pass.

# 7. Bottom-line review outcome

The external review validated the central internal diagnosis:

> AUREVANE's strongest originality already exists in its relationships among Becoming, continuity, consequence, history and player identity.

The correct response to generic-fantasy risk is **not more lore nouns**.

It is better execution of:

- ordinary civilization;
- material culture;
- recurring relationships;
- historically layered places;
- clear tactical identity;
- rule-bound contradiction;
- consequence memory;
- selective visual/audio motifs;
- philosophical conflict grounded in specific human harm.

The authoritative synthesis is `docs/AUREVANE_IDENTITY_CONSULTANT_SYNTHESIS.md`.
