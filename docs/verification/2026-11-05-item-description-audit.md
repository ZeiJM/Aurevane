## Engine trace and evidence

`parseRuntimeItemDefinition` validates every source and candidate. `captureEquipmentAbilitySource` captures each exact equipment definition; `commitCombatAbilityCommand` executes Kindle's actual authored manual behavior and pays its real AP cost. `combat-tag-registry.ts`, `combat-damage-up.ts`, damage settlement and the shared status lifecycle determine the final packet behavior. Absence of `damageTypes` intentionally means All Damage under the approved [Damage Up scope specification](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-damage-up-types-design.md).

Focused battle regression `starter-item-damage-scope.test.ts` casts actual equipped Kindle, attacks with Fire/Water/Ice/Storm/plain/Pierce/Pierce+Fire packets, checks JSON capture reload, checks one boost per matching packet, and checks Dispel and ordinary two-turn expiry with the ongoing passive retained. 100-point packets become 121/100/100/100/100/100/121 with v4 Kindle plus the Fire passive; the historical absent-scope capture remains 121/110/110/110/110/110/121. Removing or expiring Kindle leaves only the passive: 110/100/100/100/100/100/110. This separates scope correctness from balance acceptance.
