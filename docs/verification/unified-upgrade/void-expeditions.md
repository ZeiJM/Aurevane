# Void expeditions and ferry review

The Void is a separate mainland-inaccessible landmass inspired by the established continuity frontier. Its public silhouette is masked on the World Atlas. The existing Last Reliable Survey remains a separate original route. The mainland sector and road fingerprint is an explicit regression gate.

The timetable uses a single hull and real UTC. Outbound sails at the hour and return sails at half past. Each passage takes five minutes and boarding opens five minutes ahead. Players confirm a free fare for now. The ship makes the scheduled crossing without passengers. The moving globe icon uses a coarse illustration, with private landing coordinates and passenger records omitted from the observer view.

The authoritative voyage is keyed by direction and departure. Each has a walkable deck shared by the riders on that sailing. The same state version, selected-character ownership and replay rules used by World protect boarding and disembarking. Crossing time comes from the database, and the first eligible read after arrival settles the player at the dock. Active battles and other blocking activity defer relocation while the ferry timetable continues.

## The shifting continent

The initial 13 districts form one connected but separate exploration map. Shift cadence defaults to 24 real hours and is editable in World Environment. A sailing reserves a common outbound phase. Away from the landing, the phase remains pinned for the visit. At the exact landing, an idle player can adopt the current phase at the same legal coordinates. Travel, combat and old saved positions retain their walkability and legality.

The rendering direction combines quiet refuge, ruined continuity, fractured plateaus, rare natural phenomena and open wilderness. Terrain and discovery density vary across districts. Scenic art, route authority, available interactions, persistent discoveries and eligible rewards have separate checks.
