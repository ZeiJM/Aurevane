# Aurevane owner audit

Use a disposable test environment and test accounts. Record the test environment, source commit, browser, device, expected behavior, observed behavior and a capture for failures. The review can mark a row complete once the associated gameplay works on the candidate.

## Ferry, Void and regional identity

| Audit | What to do | Expected |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Port access | Reach the ferry station on the eastern mainland loop. Try a forged Board request away from the dock, while walking and during battle. | Ferryman is local. The server rejects boarding from another tile, while moving or while blocked. Fare is free. |
| Timetable | Observe the mainland departure at the hour and reverse departure at half past. Board within the five-minute window. Try leaving just before and just after departure. | Both directions run hourly. A queued rider may step off before departure. Travel takes five real minutes and uses the server clock independently of frozen game time. |

| Recovery | Close a rider's tab through arrival, then re-enter the world. Start an eligible non-world battle before arrival and check the return. | The passage is restored or settled once. Active battles keep their scene, appearance and rules; relocation settles when safe. |
