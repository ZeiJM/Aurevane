modules compile to the existing event/action/causal-relation interpreter, retaining every primitive
and its exact grouping. Source/recipient scope is a trigger parameter. Unknown historical or custom

Generic Primed means at least one genuine, living, current Controlled capture is prepared. It is
not an independently grantable status. The shared amber PRI rail row derives from that same state

anchor or inventory. Historical ability-specific prime requirements keep their exact meaning.

A resource-change requirement uses an immutable native mutation witness and the actual before/after
resource delta. Restorative Current requires MP gained, not merely a resource_changed label or a
zero recovery. Its minimum gain is editable.

## Barrier lifecycle

Each timed pool captures its own clock. Instant protection expires after the affected character's
completed turns, after periodic effects. Normal/Delayed protection begins its complete round clock
on activation and expires at the appropriate later round boundary, before new grants activate.
Partial absorption, independent grants and JSON reload preserve clocks. Original Covert command
visibility remains attached to expiry receipts. Chronicle distinguishes expiry from shattering.

Omitted duration in historical payloads/pools remains until depleted; elapsed clocks cannot be
invented for existing untimed pools. New battles capture the published temporary protection.
