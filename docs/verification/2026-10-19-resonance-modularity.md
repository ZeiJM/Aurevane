## Barrier lifecycle

Each timed pool captures its own clock. Instant protection expires after the affected character's
completed turns, after periodic effects. Normal/Delayed protection begins its complete round clock
on activation and expires at the appropriate later round boundary, before new grants activate.
Partial absorption, independent grants and JSON reload preserve clocks. Original Covert command
visibility remains attached to expiry receipts. Chronicle distinguishes expiry from shattering.

Omitted duration in historical payloads/pools remains until depleted; elapsed clocks cannot be
invented for existing untimed pools. New battles capture the published temporary protection.
