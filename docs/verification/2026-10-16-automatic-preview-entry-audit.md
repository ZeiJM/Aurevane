## Native integration boundary

A flat list of effects or a low-HP final state cannot substitute for the actual parent event. A displayed reference Skill alone does not establish that its action occurred or that its cost/HP/status mutation settled. Existing pure Manual/Ongoing forecasts do not dispatch commitment hooks. The Automatic preview must deliberately represent the scenario and its parent/child sequence, preserve input state and captures, and disclose any sandbox execution/RNG rather than silently inheriting the current no-RNG claim.
