# RETIRED — Mantle Roadmap

**Status:** Retired by Owner decision on 2026-09-20.

Mantle is no longer a canonical AUREVANE power family. The supernatural roadmap now uses **Ascension / Ascended** and **Severence / Severed**.

The former Mantle roadmap belongs to the Severence design space and must not be implemented as a separate third subsystem.

Use:

- `docs/SUPERNATURAL_PATHS.md` for the canonical supernatural model;
- `docs/ROADMAP.md` for phase sequencing;
- `docs/GAME_MASTER_PLAN.md` for the overall product contract.

**Severence** is the intentional AUREVANE spelling.
