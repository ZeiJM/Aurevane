# AUREVANE — Frontier, Rekindling Replayability & Expanded PvP Addendum

**Status:** Owner-approved authoritative design refinement subordinate only to `docs/GAME_MASTER_PLAN.md` and complementary to `docs/LORE_BIBLE.md`, `docs/WORLD.md`, `docs/PROGRESSION_RETENTION.md`, `docs/PVP_SPECTATION_COLOSSEUM.md`, `docs/HOMESTEAD_WORLD_NAVIGATION.md`, and `docs/ROADMAP.md`.

**Direction approved:** 2026-08-19.

This addendum develops three connected long-term directions:

1. Rekindling must make a repeated level journey feel meaningfully different rather than asking players to replay the same six-month route;
2. the authored known world eventually opens into a vast shifting outer frontier tied to the existing Unchosen / Unmoored / Closed Horizon mythology and a distant supernatural threat;
3. PvP architecture supports more formats than only fixed 1v1 and 2v2 while keeping 1v1 the standard competitive baseline and avoiding population fragmentation.

Where older planning lists only 1v1/2v2 as the total PvP shape, this addendum expands the mature PvP direction. Where older Rekindling wording can be read as a near-identical re-level, this addendum requires replay variance. It does **not** change the current approximately 180-day first-cycle / Rekindling pacing rule by itself.

Current build terminology remains Primary Discipline, optional mastered Secondary Discipline, Discipline Skills, Resonance, Essence, Soulmark, Soul-Severed, Mantle, Equipment Skills, and bounded Veteran Edge.

---

# PART I — REKINDLING MUST CREATE A NEW JOURNEY

## 1. Core Rule

Rekindling is AUREVANE's prestige system, but prestige must not mean:

> reset level → repeat the same quests → repeat the same route → wait another six months.

The intended fantasy is:

> **The same character begins another era of Becoming, carrying history forward while the route back to mastery changes.**

A later cycle should feel familiar enough that the player's accumulated knowledge matters, but different enough that the player is not mentally replaying a solved checklist.

## 2. Preserve the Long-Form Journey, Change Its Shape

The existing production direction remains a long-form cycle with Horizon milestones and a roughly 180-day default before another full Rekindling eligibility.

What changes between cycles is the **path through the journey**.

A Rekindled cycle should be allowed to vary through a controlled combination of:

- different early and midgame progression routes;
- alternate quest chains and veteran-only quest variants;
- different regional emphasis;
- cycle-specific world objectives;
- optional build-development goals;
- different Expedition qualification routes;
- alternate boss/rite requirements;
- lore routes that reveal different evidence;
- changing live-world events;
- PvP or co-op emphasis chosen by the player where appropriate;
- frontier exploration objectives once the outer frontier exists;
- character-specific reactions to prior-cycle history.

The game should not require every Rekindled character to consume exactly the same content in exactly the same order.

## 3. Cycle Focus

A Rekindled character may select a **Cycle Focus** or equivalent data-driven route modifier after the initial Rekindling ceremony.

The final player-facing name is not locked by this addendum.

Possible focuses include:

- **Combat / Champion** — greater emphasis on difficult authored fights, elite hunts and mastery trials;
- **Explorer** — greater emphasis on world discovery, frontier charting and rare locations;
- **Delver** — Expedition-heavy qualification and unusual run conditions;
- **Scholar** — Archive reconstruction, contradictions, relics and hidden story routes;
- **Challenger** — PvP milestones and competitive mastery where population supports it;
- **Steward** — guild, nation, social and community-world objectives after those systems exist.

A Focus is not a permanent class and should not lock the player out of the rest of the game. It changes the route, bonus objectives, qualification mix and presentation of the cycle.

## 4. Veteran Shortcuts Without Trivializing Progression

Rekindled players should not be forced through beginner friction they have already conclusively mastered.

Examples of acceptable veteran conveniences:

- skip or compress basic tutorial explanations;
- veteran dialogue that acknowledges prior knowledge;
- faster requalification for already-understood basic travel/services;
- reduced repetition of introductory fetch/tutorial quests;
- alternate veteran versions of early encounters;
- accelerated reacquisition inside already-mastered low-risk progression bands where balance allows;
- account/character knowledge surfaces remembering previously discovered systems;
- optional direct routing toward a chosen Cycle Focus.

These shortcuts must not become free endgame progression, instant Discipline Mastery, automatic rare loot, free PvP rank, free Expedition clears, or automatic story decisions.

## 5. Echo Encounters & Hall of Selves

The existing **Hall of Selves** should become mechanically useful, not only commemorative.

Prior-cycle snapshots may power optional **Echo Encounters** such as:

- duel a controlled reconstruction of a previous build;
- fight alongside a prior-cycle echo in a special solo scenario;
- confront a distorted Unchosen version of the character;
- solve a tactical trial built around the weaknesses of the player's former setup;
- revisit a prior major choice through an explicitly non-canon possibility-space scenario;
- compare old and new build identities in a Rekindling rite.

Echoes never grant the client arbitrary control of historical combat state. They are server-authored projections generated from safe stored Hall-of-Selves data and current legal combat definitions.

This also creates a strong thematic bridge to Aurevane's obsession with roads not taken.

## 6. Rekindling Story Recognition

The world should recognize that a Rekindled character is not a new adventurer.

Possible reactions include:

- veteran-only NPC dialogue;
- altered introductions;
- recognition from institutions, guilds, nations and recurring NPCs;
- Aurevane reacting to the character's repeated acts of self-reinvention;
- Closed Star concern about characters who repeatedly rebuild themselves without losing identity;
- lore that interprets Rekindling differently across cultures;
- alternate quest branches based on prior-cycle Chronicle history.

Rekindling should strengthen AUREVANE's central theme: **change without erasing continuity**.

## 7. Rekindling Anti-Fatigue Gate

Before mature Rekindling is considered ready, validate that:

- the second cycle is not simply the first cycle with faster numbers;
- players can choose meaningfully different progression emphasis;
- known tutorials can be bypassed safely;
- repeated mandatory content is kept low;
- Hall-of-Selves history produces visible value;
- prior choices are acknowledged;
- the cycle still contains real progression rather than becoming an automated prestige lap;
- returning to Level 1/low level does not make the veteran feel socially or mechanically erased.

# PART II — THE VERGE & THE UNCHARTED

## 8. World Structure

The current authored world remains the stable core of AUREVANE.

At the far perimeter of civilized and mapped territory, the Atlas eventually reaches a deliberately incomplete boundary.

Working terminology:

- **The Verge** — the stable authored outer boundary / final mapped frontier of the known world;
- **The Uncharted** — the shifting territory beyond the Verge;
- **World's Edge** — acceptable colloquial NPC/player language, not required as the system name.

These are working player-facing names and may receive a later dedicated lore/naming pass without changing the system design.

The ordinary Atlas should visually communicate that known geography ends. It should not pretend the entire world has already been cleanly surveyed.

## 9. Entering the Uncharted

The Uncharted is a separate exploration layer rather than an infinitely scrollable extension of the normal Atlas.

At an eligible Verge crossing, the player receives an explicit transition such as:

```text
THE VERGE
Beyond this point, stable maps become unreliable.

[Cross into the Uncharted]
[Remain in the Known World]
```

The final copy depends on lore progression and must not spoil the Closed Horizon / Aurevane truth before the character is allowed to know it.

Entry can require level, Horizon, story, equipment, party or other authored eligibility depending on depth/content.

## 10. Near-Infinite Without Pretending to Generate Infinity

The Uncharted should **feel effectively unbounded** while remaining technically controlled.

Use a server-authoritative, deterministic, seeded frontier made from authored content modules rather than one truly infinite procedural simulation.

The frontier may be composed from:

- terrain sectors;
- travel nodes;
- encounter templates;
- ruins;
- anomaly sites;
- camps;
- hazards;
- battle scenes;
- lore fragments;
- rare authored landmarks;
- temporary world-event sites;
- deep gates / boss locations.

The system expands by adding more authored modules, biomes, rules and depth bands over time.

## 11. The Daily Shift

The Uncharted's connective geography changes on a regular world cycle, with **daily** as the initial design target.

The important rule is that the shift should be **shared world truth**, not random per-player noise.

A production-friendly model:

```text
SERVER FRONTIER CYCLE
+ DATE / VERSION / EVENT STATE
=> DETERMINISTIC UNCHARTED SEED
=> SHARED DAILY FRONTIER LAYOUT
```

Players on the same world/server can therefore discuss what exists today, share discoveries, coordinate routes and race toward mysteries.

The next cycle changes paths, ordinary points of interest, encounter distribution and some temporary landmarks.

## 12. Anchors vs Shifting Geography

Not everything should disappear every day.

Separate the frontier into:

### Anchored locations

Rare authored places whose identity persists even if the route to them changes.

Examples:

- ancient observatories;
- impossible ruins;
- sealed Great Vane-related structures;
- recurring deep camps;
- major threat sites;
- long-form quest destinations.

### Drifting locations

Temporary or reconfigured locations created by the active frontier seed.

Examples:

- enemy camps;
- anomalies;
- resource sites;
- small ruins;
- temporary passages;
- event nodes;
- rare encounters;
- minor lore sites.

This allows the Uncharted to be mysterious without making long-form storytelling impossible.

## 13. Lore Explanation — Reality Is Less Anchored Beyond the Known World

This direction should build on existing canon rather than invent a disconnected magical wilderness.

The Eight Great Vanes helped anchor the regions that became the stable known world. The deepest outer territory lies farther from the strongest stabilizing influence of those structures and is more vulnerable to pressure from the **Unchosen**.

The player should not learn the full explanation immediately.

Early interpretations can include:

- magnetic/cartographic disturbance;
- ancient weather;
- supernatural migration;
- unreliable maps;
- vanished roads;
- folklore about the world's edge.

Later evidence may reveal that the frontier literally has difficulty agreeing with itself about what geography is real.

The Uncharted therefore becomes a large-scale gameplay expression of the existing Unmoored mythology.

## 14. Why Players Go There

The Uncharted needs purpose beyond “random map with stronger monsters.”

Core motivations can include:

- charting temporary routes;
- discovering anchored landmarks;
- recovering Archive evidence;
- locating rare equipment and materials;
- hunting frontier elites and bosses;
- encountering rare Soulmark/Severance-related rites where appropriate;
- discovering rare **Disciplines** through authored sidegrade acquisition paths;
- closing or stabilizing breaches;
- rescuing lost explorers;
- escorting survey teams;
- tracking supernatural migrations;
- discovering temporary Expedition entrances;
- participating in community mapping objectives;
- finding evidence that predicts threats entering the known world;
- earning social prestige for difficult discoveries;
- qualifying for deeper frontier access across long-term character progression.

Rare frontier Disciplines must be **different**, not strictly stronger than ordinary Disciplines.

## 15. Frontier Familiarity / Legendary Status

A character can become known for genuine mastery of the Uncharted.

Use a persistent achievement/renown layer such as **Frontier Familiarity**, **Verge Renown**, or another final name chosen later.

Progress should come from diverse accomplishments rather than one grind bar:

- depth reached;
- distinct cycles successfully explored;
- anchored sites discovered;
- rare threats defeated;
- difficult extractions;
- lore reconstructions completed;
- routes charted;
- community first-witness achievements;
- deep objectives stabilized;
- frontier bosses cleared;
- long-term exploration milestones across multiple seasons/cycles.

The highest public recognition may use a title such as **Legend of the Verge** / **Frontier Legend** after final naming review.

## 16. What “Legendary Power” Should Mean

Frontier mastery may unlock meaningful power, but it should use existing AUREVANE build systems rather than casually creating a new unlimited power layer.

Preferred reward channels:

- unique **Veteran Edge choices** after appropriate Rekindling milestones;
- rare but balanced frontier Disciplines;
- frontier-origin equipment and Equipment Skills;
- rare Soulmarks or supernatural progression opportunities where lore supports them;
- specialized PvE traversal/forecast advantages inside the Uncharted;
- cosmetic transformations, titles, profile treatments and Homestead trophies;
- access to advanced frontier quests and encounters.

Do **not** use ordinary-player “Anomaly Skills” as a new generic reward category. The current **Anomaly Character** concept is an exceptional GAME OWNER-only supernatural override and should not be blurred with normal progression.

If a frontier-earned effect is too strong for standard ranked competition, ranked rules normalize or disable it explicitly.

## 17. Frontier Knowledge Advantages

Some of the best veteran advantages are informational rather than raw stats.

Examples:

- reveal an additional nearby route candidate;
- identify whether a landmark is anchored or drifting;
- estimate anomaly danger more accurately;
- gain better extraction forecasts;
- recognize specific Horizonless signs;
- unlock safer camp/return options;
- retain more useful cartographic annotations between cycles;
- identify rare POI categories without exposing exact hidden rewards.

This makes experienced explorers feel genuinely experienced.

## 18. Daily Shift Safety

A daily world change must never delete a player's active effort because a clock rolled over.

Required behavior:

- active party/session can remain pinned to its current frontier seed until a safe transition;
- rewards already earned remain authoritative;
- no character is killed or loses inventory because the daily seed changed;
- reconnect knows which frontier version owns the active session;
- stale sessions have a safe expiry/extraction policy;
- server controls the reset boundary;
- client clock is never authority.

## 19. Anti-FOMO Rule

The Uncharted can create strong “what is out there today?” curiosity without making essential power permanently missable.

Good ephemeral rewards:

- first-witness recognition;
- Chronicle entries;
- titles;
- cosmetics;
- unique screenshots/memories;
- temporary route discoveries;
- leaderboard placements.

Important build-enabling Disciplines, Soulmarks, equipment or permanent combat options must recur or have alternate acquisition routes.

# PART III — THE DISTANT SUPERNATURAL THREAT

## 20. Purpose

AUREVANE benefits from a threat that can be **felt long before it is fully understood or directly fought**.

This threat should create:

- dread at the world's edge;
- unexplained disappearances;
- strange migration patterns;
- altered frontier landmarks;
- evidence brought back by explorers;
- rare incursions into stable territory;
- reasons for nations/guilds/explorers to care about the frontier;
- a persistent story engine that can grow over years.

It must remain original to AUREVANE and support, rather than replace, Aurevane as the principal long-form antagonist.

## 21. Working Threat Concept — The Horizonless

**The Horizonless** is the recommended working name for a distant supernatural people/host encountered only in the deepest outer frontier at first.

The final name is deliberately not locked until a dedicated lore pass.

The Horizonless are not ordinary monsters and are not necessarily one biologically uniform species.

A useful foundational mystery is that they appear to be beings whose relationship with a single stable history has become damaged or transformed.

Possible traits:

- contradictory physical details between sightings;
- individuals remembered differently by different witnesses;
- equipment that looks authored by cultures that never existed;
- coordinated behavior across impossible distances;
- camps that appear older than the route leading to them;
- symbols related to open/closed horizons without immediately explaining allegiance;
- entities whose combat identity can shift within controlled rules;
- processions or migrations that seem to move toward stable reality.

## 22. Do Not Make Them Simple Aurevane Minions

The Horizonless should not flatten the story into “Aurevane has an army.”

Possible truths can remain unresolved for a long time:

- some may seek the Great Opening;
- some may consider Aurevane a liberator;
- some may fear her;
- some may be fleeing a deeper collapse of their own reality;
- some may want the known world's stabilizing anchors destroyed;
- some may want them preserved for reasons the player does not yet understand;
- different groups may have incompatible histories of the same origin.

Their existence demonstrates what prolonged instability between actuality and possibility can do to cultures and individuals.

Aurevane remains the principal mythic antagonist; the Horizonless are a persistent civilization/threat born from or shaped by the same cosmological problem.

## 23. Threat Escalation

Escalate over long periods:

### Stage A — Signs

- missing survey parties;
- impossible footprints;
- altered ruins;
- contradictory maps;
- distant silhouettes;
- unexplained frontier warnings.

### Stage B — Contact

- isolated scouts;
- strange diplomacy;
- hostile encounters;
- captured artifacts;
- evidence of organized culture.

### Stage C — Incursions

- breaches into outer known-world regions;
- live world crises;
- attacks on strategic frontier locations;
- migration waves;
- Great Vane instability correlated with their movement.

### Stage D — Persistent Conflict

- deep campaigns;
- guild/nation objectives;
- Frontier Expeditions;
- major bosses/commanders;
- competing Horizonless factions;
- direct connection to War of Possible Worlds story arcs.

Do not rush Stage D into early launch merely because it is planned.

## 24. Threat Gameplay Identity

Horizonless combat should express unstable possibility through fair typed mechanics such as:

- temporary alternate combat profiles;
- controlled echo units;
- terrain that changes between predefined states;
- actions that reveal several possible outcomes before one resolves;
- memory/identity-themed statuses;
- coordinated “procession” formations;
- route-control mechanics;
- Unmoored battle objects;
- enemies whose known tactics change across frontier cycles while remaining learnable.

No mechanic may rely on secretly changing rules after the player commits without readable counterplay.

# PART IV — EXPANDED PvP FORMAT FRAMEWORK

## 25. Core PvP Direction

AUREVANE should not architect PvP as permanently limited to exactly two fixed formats.

The mature combat/match framework should support:

- **1v1**;
- **2v2**;
- **3v3**;
- **Clash:** 1–3 players on Team A vs 1–3 players on Team B, including intentionally uneven casual/custom matchups such as 1v2 or 2v3;
- **three-way free-for-all:** 1v1v1;
- future event/tournament formats built on the same participant/team abstraction where justified.

This is a capability direction, not permission to launch every queue simultaneously.

## 26. 1v1 Remains the Standard

**1v1 remains the default competitive reference mode.**

Reasons:

- easiest queue to populate;
- clearest individual skill signal;
- easiest balance/readability baseline;
- strongest foundation for learning competitive combat;
- minimal dependency on team availability.

Ranked 1v1 should be the first persistent public ranked queue unless real population/validation proves a better ordering.

## 27. 2v2 and 3v3

Team modes add build-composition depth.

2v2 remains especially valuable because it creates synergy/counterplay without the battlefield becoming too difficult to read.

3v3 becomes the largest standard team-vs-team mode currently planned.

For both:

- each player controls their own character;
- team composition matters;
- matchmaking and rating are mode-specific;
- battle UI must remain readable on desktop/mobile;
- reconnect/timeout rules handle multiple human participants;
- spectation/Colosseum systems understand the mode.

Permanent ranked 2v2/3v3 queues only open when population supports acceptable matchmaking health.

## 28. Clash — Flexible 1–3 vs 1–3

**Clash** is the working label for casual/custom two-team battles allowing one to three players per side.

Examples:

```text
1v1
1v2
1v3
2v2
2v3
3v3
```

The final player-facing name may change.

Uneven team sizes are inherently asymmetric and therefore should begin as:

- direct challenges;
- custom matches;
- social/guild events;
- special exhibitions;
- explicitly advertised challenge rules.

Do not silently present 1v3 as ordinary fair ranked matchmaking.

If future events use handicaps or special objectives to balance uneven teams, those rules must be public, deterministic and versioned rather than hidden stat manipulation.

## 29. Three-Way Clash — 1v1v1

AUREVANE should support a genuine three-sided battle format where each of three players is independently hostile to the other two.

This requires combat architecture that does not assume every battle has exactly `TEAM_A` and `TEAM_B`.

Initial use should be casual/event/custom until validated because three-way PvP introduces:

- temporary alliances;
- kingmaking;
- focus-fire politics;
- turn-order pressure;
- victory-condition complexity;
- rating/scoring complexity.

Possible victory models include:

- last character standing;
- score from objectives + eliminations;
- bounded round/timer with transparent scoring;
- special event rules.

Do not create ranked 1v1v1 until the scoring system produces a credible competitive signal.

## 30. Population-Gated Queue Policy

The current product-strategy rule remains:

> **A healthy single queue is better than five empty queues.**

Therefore:

- 1v1 can remain permanently available first;
- 2v2/3v3 ranked may run only when population supports them;
- some modes may rotate by day/week/season;
- direct/custom Clash can remain available without fragmenting automatic matchmaking;
- 1v1v1 can appear as an event/Colosseum format;
- tournaments can temporarily concentrate population into specific formats.

Queue availability is an operational decision driven by telemetry.

## 31. Mode-Specific Ratings & Recognition

Do not pretend one rating measures every format equally.

Potential independent records:

- 1v1 rating/rank;
- 2v2 team-mode rating;
- 3v3 team-mode rating;
- tournament history;
- Clash exhibition record;
- 1v1v1 event achievements.

Some social profile surfaces can summarize these without turning every mode into a permanent ranked ladder.

## 32. Colosseum Integration

The existing Colosseum/spectator direction expands naturally to these modes.

Listings should understand:

- participant count;
- number of teams;
- mode name;
- ranked/casual/custom/event status;
- public visibility;
- delayed spectation policy;
- tournament/event metadata.

A 1v1v1 featured match should not require a separate spectator stack from a 3v3 match.

## 33. PvP Architecture Gate

Before the mature PvP framework is considered ready:

- battle membership supports a stable team/faction identifier rather than binary-team assumptions;
- standard combat rules can determine friend/foe legality for 2+ teams;
- targeting, AoE, summons, statuses, objectives and victory conditions behave correctly with multiple teams;
- turn order remains fair/readable with up to six human-controlled characters in the planned standard team modes;
- disconnect/forfeit logic handles team and free-for-all cases;
- spectation/replay records all participants correctly;
- rankings are mode-aware;
- asymmetric Clash is never mislabeled as ordinary ranked fairness;
- queue activation remains population-gated.

# PART V — HOW THESE SYSTEMS CONNECT

## 34. Rekindling + Frontier

The frontier is one of the strongest tools for making later Rekindling cycles different.

Examples:

- first cycle: player only reaches outer Verge content;
- later cycle: Explorer Focus sends them deeper into the Uncharted;
- a Rekindled character recognizes landmarks ordinary characters cannot interpret;
- Hall-of-Selves echoes appear in possibility-distorted frontier sites;
- rare frontier Veteran Edge choices require both Rekindling and frontier accomplishments;
- different frontier cycles expose different lore evidence and bosses.

## 35. Frontier + Living World

The Uncharted can act as an early-warning system for the known world.

What explorers discover today can foreshadow tomorrow's world event:

```text
frontier migration detected
  ↓
World Pulse warning
  ↓
outer-region NPC reactions
  ↓
community preparation objective
  ↓
incursion / crisis
  ↓
Chronicle aftermath
```

This makes frontier exploration matter to players who never personally travel there.

## 36. Frontier + Guilds / Nations

Later systems may include:

- guild charting expeditions;
- nation-sponsored frontier surveys;
- competition over first discoveries;
- cooperative defense of frontier gates;
- nation-specific interpretation of Horizonless contact;
- public Chronicle recognition for major discoveries.

Do not turn frontier ownership into permanent territorial monopolies that lock ordinary players out of core content.

## 37. Frontier + Homestead

Homesteads remain safe personal spaces and are not raided by frontier enemies.

Frontier achievement can feed Homestead identity through:

- trophies;
- maps;
- display relics;
- cosmetic furnishings;
- rare ambience/themes;
- visitor-visible achievement objects.

This preserves the safe-home fantasy while letting exploration history become visible.

## 38. Frontier + PvP

The Uncharted should not become open-world forced PvP by default.

Potential competitive connections are explicit:

- frontier-themed arena maps;
- rotating PvP event rules;
- tournaments tied to world events;
- competition for first discovery via PvE/community metrics;
- optional sanctioned challenge sites.

Players should not lose rare frontier progress because another player can casually gank them at a transition point unless a future explicitly approved opt-in mode says otherwise.

# FINAL DESIGN PRINCIPLES

1. **Rekindling repeats the character journey, not the content checklist.**
2. **The Uncharted feels infinite through changing authored combinations, not meaningless random generation.**
3. **The daily frontier is shared world truth so community knowledge matters.**
4. **Stable anchors make long-form lore possible inside shifting geography.**
5. **Frontier mastery grants identity, access, sidegrade build options and bounded advantages—not mandatory raw-stat supremacy.**
6. **Normal players do not receive the GAME OWNER-only Anomaly Character exception through frontier progression.**
7. **The distant supernatural threat extends existing Unchosen/Unmoored mythology and does not replace Aurevane as the principal antagonist.**
8. **PvP supports flexible participant/team structures, but only healthy modes become permanent queues.**
9. **1v1 remains the competitive baseline.**
10. **All new systems remain server-authoritative, versioned, replay-safe, spoiler-aware and operable through appropriate future Master Panel tools.**
