# ART-DISC-002 — Ironfist combat media

Status: requested; interim vector symbols supplied for the test release.

Scope: eight Ironfist Technique icons, Hundredfold Rush Essence and five mixed-pair Resonance icons from `packages/game-core/src/combat/ironfist-content.ts`. Keep the stable icon/audio/VFX hooks when replacing assets.

Visual contract: mature tactical fantasy, amber and worn leather/cloth, clear wrapped-hand gestures, restrained impact arcs, readable silhouettes at 64px. Differentiate rising strike, ground sweep, breathing stance, defensive palm, braced landing, downward knuckle, advancing palm and wounded resilience. Hundredfold Rush shows ordered impacts rather than a literal hundred limbs. Mixed icons combine the two Discipline motifs without a third unrelated identity. No text, franchise likeness, malformed anatomy or excessive bloom.

Audio/VFX request: concise cloth/footwork/impact cues; ordered hit feedback for the Essence; clear setup/payoff accents for Resonance. Respect global mute, existing category controls and reduced motion. The current shared battle feedback remains in use; unique production recordings/illustrations are not claimed complete.

Provenance: interim deterministic vector renderer already owned by the repository; authored IDs and amber palette added in the Phase-4 ticket. Final generated/commissioned candidates require Art/Audio Bible review, runtime derivatives and provenance before replacement.
